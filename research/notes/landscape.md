# Bread and fermentation software landscape

Surveyed 24 November 2026. Scope: public descriptions, listed screens and selected source code, not a complete hands-on comparison. Product statements are vendor claims unless marked otherwise; prices and functionality can change by country/version. `?` means not established, **not absent**. English-language pages were read; language coverage beyond that is unknown unless noted. No claim of market-wide uniqueness follows from this sample.

A timer or retrospective comparison is not itself a predictive model or calibration.

## Product observations and limitations

- **BakingSmart**: the [Play description](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.adisasta.myrecipe) specifies automatic hydration, percentages and scaling, milk-water replacement, timers attached to recipe steps, per-stage ambient/dough temperatures over 18 stages, bake comparison charts, local-focused use and XML/PDF export. It explicitly says no mandatory cloud accounts or social feed. The model behind calculations, supported languages, iOS availability and predictive calibration were not established. Stage logs are valuable data, not proof of an automatic kinetic model. [Novita’s site](https://www.novitalistyani.com/) identifies it and gives the one-time price.
- **Loafy**, not “Loafi”: [The Bread Code](https://www.the-bread-code.io/) links the web tools, community, planner and journals. The [iOS listing](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/loafy-sourdough-bread-baking/id6804445252) describes logged conditions, comparative analytics, named starters, guided timers, sync, and answers with chapter references to *The Sourdough Framework*. Those citations refer to a book, not necessarily to scientific primary studies; no automatic timing calibration was verified. Strong learning loop; account-based sync entails a different privacy trade-off to device-only tools.
- **BreadStorm**: [Baker’s Hundred](https://bakershundred.com/) imports exported `.bun` files, scales multiple levain/poolish builds, distinguishes build flour from final-mix flour, prints/exports CSV and states processing is local. It cannot open the BreadStorm3.xml library. This is evidence about the **viewer and a sample BreadStorm export**, not evidence that all viewer capabilities or prices belong to the original Mac product. The former breadstorm.com request redirected to a domain marketplace and did not complete; current original product availability unverified.
- **The Perfect Loaf**: [DDT guide, “How to Calculate”](https://www.theperfectloaf.com/the-importance-of-dough-temperature-in-baking/) discloses the four-factor equation including levain and friction and recommends measuring actual dough temperature. Its bulk table is explicitly illustrative and recipe-specific, not a generally validated model. Companion [water calculator](https://www.theperfectloaf.com/water-temperature-calculator/) and linked starter calculator split tasks cleanly. Fahrenheit examples and Celsius conversions appear in the guide.
- **King Arthur**: [baker’s percentage guide](https://www.kingarthurbaking.com/blog/2022/04/28/what-is-bakers-math-and-how-can-i-use-it-in-my-everyday-bread-baking) and [dough temperature reference](https://www.kingarthurbaking.com/pro/reference/dough-temperature) both returned HTTP 406 to the reader. Their exact calculator features, price and model therefore remain unverified; these are references to revisit, not negative findings.
- **Brød & Taylor**: [manufacturer homepage](https://brodandtaylor.com/) describes temperature-controlled starter equipment and a proofer with cold retarding and remote monitoring. A software calculator and its inputs could not be verified from that page. Physical temperature control is not proof of software fermentation prediction; equipment pricing and app/platform unknown.
- **PizzApp+**: the [official iOS listing](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/pizzapp/id1228158792) confirms ingredient and yeast suggestions, save/share, version history including water-temperature calculation, and examples of multilingual support. A [third-party examination of v1.9](https://www.housegardenhobby.com/pizzapp-guide-pizza-dough/) reports yeast type, hydration, room and cold time/temperature, poolish/biga, fixed biga hydration and manual yeast multiplier, plus offline operation; these details were not independently tested. Its report says biga yeast does not change with time/temperature. Individual App Store reviewers praise quick scaling but report ads/ad-block startup trouble, limited direct flour entry, and potential negative remaining ingredients in a poolish edge case; these are anecdotes from specific versions, not verified present defects. A poolish fraction can legitimately be defined relative to flour **or** water: label the denominator and reject infeasible quantities rather than adopting a reviewer’s preferred definition as universal.
- **PizzaBlab**: its [calculator and fermentation notes](https://www.pizzablab.com/calculators/pizza-dough-calculator/) show totals and separate preferment/final dough, yeast recommendation based on time/temperature and cold procedure; it states total fermentation begins at end of kneading and ends at bake. It notes yeast vitality, temperature drift and final dough temperature as sources of deviation. Formula/source validation and exact temperature profile handling were not established. Grams and spoon measures, print/share and a separate DDT calculator are offered.
- **Stadler Made**: the [calculator redirects to Makerclub](https://www.stadlermade.com/pizza-calculator); it describes style, dough portions, hydration, salt, yeast types, sourdough, semolina, fat and flour strength, with advanced tools behind Plus. No fermentation algorithm or pricing was verified from the page body. Do not infer its prediction inputs from another pizza app.
- **PizzaMaking.com yeast chart / TXCraig**: the [community thread](https://www.pizzamaking.com/forum/index.php?topic=26831.0) is cited by the [third-party calculator](https://derwille1.github.io/cf-calculator/); original thread was not independently read. The calculator’s [actual source](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/derwille1/cf-calculator/main/index.html) implements hours = round(30.91 × IDY%^-0.72 × exp(-0.110 × (°F−35))), with a single constant temperature and inverse dose. It calls itself based on TXCraig’s model; original derivation and accuracy are unverified. It advises subtracting prior warm bulk, not an explicit two-phase model. **Code discrepancy:** displayed text says fresh yeast = 3 × IDY, while `YEAST_FACTORS.CY=1/3` and `fromIDY` multiply by that factor; its conversion output contradicts its own explanation. Do not copy this implementation without independent validation or assume the original thread has the same defect.
- **Sourdough Fermentation Calculator**: [calculator / “How this calculator works”](https://sourdoughfermentationcalculator.com/) accepts starting dough and ambient temperatures, hydration, starter percentage and bulk versus proof; describes temperature drift, but not a disclosed numerical equation or validation dataset. Gives estimates with visual endpoints, not guarantees. The page offers a cold-proof tool separately; an integrated multi-phase calculation was not established.
- **SourdoughGuru**: [official feature page](https://sourdough.guru/) describes backward scheduling, temperature-adjusted timing, separately shown flour/water in levain, live step timers, feed log, shareable bake cards and photo comparison. Its example labels bulk a range and says dough decides. Its AI crumb classifications are product claims, not validated diagnoses; equation and learning from outcomes are not disclosed.
- **BreadMath**: [Play listing](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.clearstackapps.breadmath&hl=en_US) describes gram/ounce switching, multiple flours, starter hydration, device-local offline recipes and photos, reminders and forward/backward timeline. Pro unlocks journal, schedule, scaling and notifications. No equation for predicting fermentation is described.
- **Flourwise**: [feature page](https://flourwise.com/) describes flour/target-weight/piece modes, poolish/biga/levain, egg and milk water, DDT, pizza yeast based on temperature and duration including cold fermentation, timers, recipes, nutrition/cost, and starter forecasts that learn from feeding history. The latter is a **starter forecast**, not demonstrated personal calibration of whole-dough fermentation. Pizza model, exact handling of successive phases, price tiers, supported platforms and scald treatment are not specified.
- **doughplan**: [README / math and roadmap](https://github.com/SaschaHeyer/doughplan) discloses mass scaling and reverse scheduling by supplied fixed offsets, aggregate shopping and a hosted product with separate DDT and starter tools. Its roadmap explicitly puts temperature-dependent levain timing in the future. Fixed offsets are not biological predictions. MIT license applies to the repository; check the exact code and license before reuse.
- **sourrr**: [README / examples](https://github.com/andrewheiss/sourrr) documents baker’s math, different starter hydration and reverse recipe calculation. The author explicitly warns that target dough weight currently adds salt beyond the target; do not import that bug into an engine. Verify the actual repository license before reuse.

## Requirements Breadator should meet

1. Accept flour mass, target total dough or portion count/weight; show live grams and baker’s percentages, rounding residuals, total mass and units. Decompose all levain/preferment flour and water, allow arbitrary hydration and multiple flour types. Show both total formula and per-stage remaining ingredients, and identify each percentage’s denominator; validate impossible allocations rather than displaying negative ingredient weights.
2. Include separate, transparent DDT calculation with/without preferment temperature, user-measured mixing/friction assumptions, Celsius/Fahrenheit and units. A computed water temperature is an estimate, not a promise of final temperature.
3. Support direct yeast and levain, conversion assumptions explicitly labeled, customizable poolish/biga/starter builds, and target time **or** dose. Cold and warm time/temperature must be separate stages, with actual dough temperatures distinguished from ambient/setpoint and cooling lag visible; show assumptions, valid ranges, uncertainty, and physical dough readiness checks.
4. Forward/backward planner with editable steps, adjustable timers and device-friendly reminders; explicitly avoid calling fixed schedule offsets predictive. Let people log the actual times, temperatures, rise endpoint, recipe and outcome, then compare bakes and track feeding histories. Enable correction of the prediction from repeated comparable results without presenting unvalidated personal fits as measured science.
5. Mobile-first usable controls and readable desktop tables; metric/imperial units, accessible non-colour-only status, French/English localization, save/export and privacy-conscious account choices. Offline support and sync should be independently designed, not assumed equivalent.

## Opportunities and evidence boundaries

- **Integrated multi-phase personalized prediction**: PizzApp’s reported RT/CT dose already covers two temperature conditions; Flourwise reports cold fermentation and SourdoughGuru adjusts timing to temperature. Loafy compares outcomes and Flourwise learns a starter forecast. What was **not established in this sampled sources** is a transparent model integrating arbitrary measured dough-temperature phases, cold cooling lag, user-calibrated bulk/proof endpoints, error ranges and disclosed validation all together. Do not claim no competitor offers it.
- **Scald-aware hydration**: Flourwise explicitly tracks non-water ingredients and preferments; no reviewed description established a stage-aware scald mass balance with evaporation or absorption. Offer gross water/flour plus explicitly defined adjusted measures, with measurable losses, rather than treating a theoretical “effective hydration” as universally measurable.
- **Evidence-linked calculations**: DDT’s equation is transparent at The Perfect Loaf; Loafy cites a book; TXCraig-derived source publishes code. None reviewed established an end-to-end, versioned scientific provenance and uncertainty/validation trail for a personalized phase model. Cite primary studies and distinguish measured data, empirical rules and extrapolation. A scientifically sourced model still needs domain-specific validation.
- **Disagreements and ambiguities**: TXCraig-derived fresh yeast code contradicts its own displayed conversion; PizzApp poolish reviewer and other tools use different denominator conventions. Time scales from one commercial-yeast pizza model must not be transferred to sourdough without evidence. A warm stage cannot simply be subtracted hour-for-hour from a cold stage unless the model supports that equivalence.

## Interface patterns worth adapting

- A single prominent result beside editable inputs, with an expandable calculation breakdown and units on every field; unlike colour-only warnings, include explanatory text.
- A total formula above nested stage cards, distinguishing quantities already included from what remains to add; make build and global percentage bases explicit ([Baker’s Hundred](https://bakershundred.com/), [PizzaBlab](https://www.pizzablab.com/calculators/pizza-dough-calculator/)).
- Let users start with the flour on hand, target dough or piece count, then optionally reveal advanced stages, rather than requiring them to reverse-engineer a target ([Flourwise](https://flourwise.com/); PizzApp reviewer feedback in its [listing](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/pizzapp/id1228158792)).
- A reverse timeline anchored to an editable bake time, with each timer pauseable and an estimate range next to sensory/volume checkpoints ([SourdoughGuru](https://sourdough.guru/)).
- Compare two logged bakes by actual temperatures, duration, rise and outcome, with explicit user consent before using logs for personalization ([BakingSmart](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.adisasta.myrecipe), [Loafy](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/loafy-sourdough-bread-baking/id6804445252)).

## Claims

### sugar

- For sweet pancakes add sugar at the end, just before cooking, so microbes do not ferment it; if added earlier, no sweetness remains after about 12 h. — `{"hours_before_sweet_lost": 12}` [breadcode-0293; SF Ch 6 Bread types; creator-rule]
- Add sugar at shaping, not at mixing; sugar added early is fermented, so little of it remains in the bread. [breadcode-0741; SF Ch 9 Mix-ins §9.3 Techniques > Incorporating seeds into the dough; creator-rule]
- Mix-ins added just before shaping are not fermented, so sugar stays sweet; mixed in at the start, most of it is consumed. [breadcode-0742; SF Ch 9 Mix-ins §9.3 Techniques > Adding before shaping; creator-rule]
- To add sugar at shaping, spread the sugar mixture over a flattened dough and roll it up so the sugar sits in layers. [breadcode-0760; SF Ch 9 Mix-ins §9.3 Techniques > Incorporating seeds into the dough; creator-rule]

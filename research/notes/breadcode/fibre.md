### fibre

- Whole-wheat flour soaked about 15 min hydrates the bran before kneading. — `{"soak_min": 15}` [breadcode-2076; yt:MPWypFY0wyE [2:56]; creator-rule]
- Whole-wheat flour absorbs noticeably more water than white flour, so whole-wheat doughs need more water than the same weight of white flour. [breadcode-2149; yt:MPWypFY0wyE [3:34]; [14:54]; creator-rule]

### fibre.seeds

- Some seeds benefit from boiling for about 10 min before adding to the dough. — `{"boil_min": 10}` [breadcode-0735; SF Ch 9 Mix-ins §9.2 Examples > Seeds and nuts; creator-rule]
- Whole rye and wheat berries are boiled for 10 min before use. — `{"boil_min": 10}` [breadcode-0736; SF Ch 9 Mix-ins §9.2 Examples > Seeds and nuts; creator-rule]
- Whole wheat or rye kernels should be soaked overnight and rinsed before adding, so they soften enough to eat. [breadcode-0737; SF Ch 9 Mix-ins §9.3 Techniques > Incorporating seeds into the dough; creator-rule]
- Unsoaked kernels: boil 10 min in hot water, then rinse in cold water before adding. — `{"boil_min": 10}` [breadcode-0738; SF Ch 9 Mix-ins §9.3 Techniques > Incorporating seeds into the dough; creator-rule]
- Seeds and nuts usable as mix-ins: cacao nibs, chia, chopped or whole almonds/hazelnuts/walnuts, flaxseed, hemp seed, poppy seed, pumpkin seed, sesame, sunflower seed, and whole rye or wheat berries. [breadcode-0757; SF Ch 9 Mix-ins §9.2 Examples > Seeds and nuts; creator-rule]
- Heavy seed content closes the crumb; in a high-hydration rye with no seeds, water evaporates in the oven and leaves an open crumb. [breadcode-2463; yt:cHwLBs9SRd4 5:03; creator-rule]
- Rye seeds soaked 12 h in water went from about 500 g dry to about 700 g. — `{"soak_h": 12, "dry_seed_g": 500, "soaked_seed_g": 700}` [breadcode-2765; yt:cN_skGXZ4Es description; anecdote]

### fibre.soakers

- A soaker is grains or seeds soaked in water for a set time, often overnight, before being added to the dough; it softens them and gives a moister crumb. [breadcode-0880; SF Glossary > Soaker; creator-rule]
- Let the rye dough ferment long enough that the seeds soak up moisture; otherwise the slices are hard to chew. [breadcode-2459; yt:cHwLBs9SRd4 2:49; creator-rule]

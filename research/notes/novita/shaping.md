# shaping — Novita Listyani / Seraphine Lishe

Paraphrased from the creators' videos (all rights reserved; facts only). Every bullet links to a claim in `data/claims-novita.jsonl` (id, video, timestamp, evidence label). Dates are video publication dates — her methods changed over time.

## Claims (14)


### shaping

- Shape a slack no-knead dough by dividing with a scraper, building surface tension in each portion, and transferring to parchment; a final 45 min rest after folding lets it become round and puffy. *[creator-rule, medium; novita-0217; [7LuO4wfL-uI 8:36–10:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7LuO4wfL-uI&t=516s); 2020-04]*
- Dimpling the dough to the bottom of the pan evens out the dough and lets it bake faster; the dimples look much shallower after baking. *[creator-rule, medium; novita-0222; [0M8A_szL2kI 8:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0M8A_szL2kI&t=488s); 2020-04]*
- Stretch the strips to 1.5 to 2 times their length before frying, without tearing. The pan diameter caps the length; she cuts 13-15 cm strips for a pan of about 24 cm. *[creator-rule, medium; novita-0796; [p5pLU6y0CpE 6:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=p5pLU6y0CpE&t=402s); 2020-06]*
- A 20-min bench rest relaxes the gluten, not to rise, so small buns shape more easily. *[creator-rule, medium; novita-0790; [T9SdsTAILj4 7:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=T9SdsTAILj4&t=420s); 2020-07]*
- Shaping controls the crumb: a gentle pat keeps large open holes, full degassing gives a denser crumb with smaller holes; bench rest 30 min before final shaping. *[creator-rule, high; novita-0204; [wl7zl__jSGI 10:18–11:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wl7zl__jSGI&t=618s); 2020-09]*
- Deflating the dough before dividing gives a more even crumb; large holes are not wanted in burger buns. *[creator-rule, medium; novita-0523; [fBtJ2ckJ9Vc 7:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=fBtJ2ckJ9Vc&t=466s); 2020-10]*
- Degas the dough thoroughly. Air trapped during mixing rises to the surface in the steamer and creates blisters. *[creator-rule, medium; novita-0777; [YR2e3Kka0TA 8:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YR2e3Kka0TA&t=485s); 2021-05]*
- Pre-shape the dough into even pieces without degassing, then bench rest 30-45 min before final shaping so the dough relaxes enough to shape. *[creator-rule, medium; novita-0704; [qy3eJV8A9ks 7:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qy3eJV8A9ks&t=450s); 2022-01]*
- In a multi-roll loaf, place adjacent rolls with spirals running in opposite directions so they push against each other and rise higher. *[creator-rule, medium; novita-0471; [WUilMHvvpCk 8:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WUilMHvvpCk&t=536s); 2022-07]*
- Roll the dough to about 0.5 cm thick, rolling from the centre outward and never to the edge; cut 8 pieces by halving three times. *[creator-rule, medium; novita-0868; [rvWSBsQLZXM 8:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rvWSBsQLZXM&t=511s); 2022-07]*
- Paw bread is shaped from a 320 g dough: one 40 g centre and eight 40 g pieces, with the remaining pieces split into 16 smaller pieces. *[creator-rule, medium; novita-0612; [B37m_f3qdWE 5:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=B37m_f3qdWE&t=308s); 2022-08]*
- Dividing into many equal pieces: divide the dough into thirds and then halves, weigh each piece, and move small amounts from heavy pieces to light ones. Target piece weight is about 37–38 g for 21 pieces; preshape and rest 5 min. *[creator-rule, high; novita-0172; [iPaBk4U33sM 10:39–13:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=iPaBk4U33sM&t=639s); 2023-06]*
- For tall baguettes, the score is made almost vertical. *[creator-rule, high; novita-0376; [KSGaNRZArBo 6:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KSGaNRZArBo&t=412s); 2023-08]*
- Divided pizza dough should relax for at least one hour before stretching; a relaxed dough stretches with less shrinkage. *[creator-rule, medium; novita-0427; [1DWHnkSwD48 22:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1DWHnkSwD48&t=1347s); 2025-02]*

# sugar — Novita Listyani / Seraphine Lishe

Paraphrased from the creators' videos (all rights reserved; facts only). Every bullet links to a claim in `data/claims-novita.jsonl` (id, video, timestamp, evidence label). Dates are video publication dates — her methods changed over time.

## Claims (20)


### sugar

- Sugar in pizza dough helps the crust brown at home oven temperatures and feeds the yeast. *[creator-rule, high; novita-0283; [f9oPmM3BklQ 3:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=f9oPmM3BklQ&t=196); 2020-05]*
- Sugar improves specific volume up to about 10% of flour; above that, quality falls. *[literature, medium; novita-0408; [1Wwvxu8C8pk 0:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1Wwvxu8C8pk&t=34s); 2021-05]*
- Above about 16% sugar on flour, yeast slows and needs more yeast to compensate; she adds about 1 g more yeast per recipe. *[creator-rule, low; novita-0323; [ACHbkOi8esY 3:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ACHbkOi8esY&t=221s); 2021-11]*
- Sugar around 2 to 3% of flour is typical for bagels: above 3% the dough may ferment too fast and the crust darken too much, below 2% the crust stays pale. *[literature, low; novita-0331; [j187SM6HwVo 4:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=j187SM6HwVo&t=291s); 2022-08]*
- Yudane 40 bread had almost twice the total saccharides of the control, with a maltose spike and slightly less glucose and fructose; Yudane 40 dough farinograph absorption ~74%; moisture rose while gassing power, gas retention and specific volume slightly fell. *[literature, high; novita-0133; [OpK1CeW9xm8 8:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OpK1CeW9xm8&t=493s); 2023-01 — cites doi:10.3136/fstr.20.1071]*
- Tangzhong 20–40% can partly replace added sugar by raising maltose (less sweet than sucrose, no fructose). *[extrapolation, low; novita-0134; [OpK1CeW9xm8 9:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OpK1CeW9xm8&t=574s); 2023-01]*
- Around 6% sugar on flour increases gas production during fermentation at first; high sugar concentrations then inhibit yeast through osmotic stress. *[literature, medium; novita-0483; [qdOHmdTTs24 13:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qdOHmdTTs24&t=789s); 2023-04]*
- Honey powder makes bread softer and less gummy after a day than sugar; fructose is more hygroscopic and honey adds springiness and cohesion. *[literature, low; novita-0539; [FVE0zv6nam4 4:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FVE0zv6nam4&t=245s); 2023-05]*
- Honey powder under 10% of flour is recommended; sensory panels preferred 5 to 10%. A liquid honey panel (31 panelists) preferred 100% honey, with lower hardness and higher moisture. *[literature, low; novita-0540; [FVE0zv6nam4 5:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FVE0zv6nam4&t=321s); 2023-05]*
- Sugar capped at about 6% (of flour weight) because studies on gas production and yeast activity suggest higher sugar slows fermentation. *[literature, low; novita-0170; [iPaBk4U33sM 1:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=iPaBk4U33sM&t=66s); 2023-06]*
- Trehalose is a non-reducing sugar, so it does not undergo the Maillard reaction with amino groups or caramelize easily; it stays pale when baked. *[literature, medium; novita-0435; [KQhgxDLsIhU 6:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KQhgxDLsIhU&t=375s); 2023-06]*
- Trehalose is about 45 percent as sweet as sucrose, has a clean finish, and does not promote dental caries in the reported studies. *[literature, medium; novita-0436; [KQhgxDLsIhU 8:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KQhgxDLsIhU&t=518s); 2023-06]*
- At 16% sugar (baker's percent), the yeast does not need an osmotolerant strain: the osmotic stress is real but not enough to cause a strongly delayed fermentation; the fermentation is just a bit longer. *[creator-rule, medium; novita-0820; [zClatNjlIPc 8:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zClatNjlIPc&t=523s); 2023-07]*
- About 6% sugar (flour basis) is enough to feed yeast for roughly 3 hours of fermentation in steamed bread; the sugar is for the yeast, not for sweetness. *[literature, medium; novita-0809; [bdjvU_fpgok 14:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bdjvU_fpgok&t=848s); 2023-12]*
- Sucrose in kombucha: reviews report 50–100 g/L; one study found higher bacterial cellulose production at 90 and 100 g/L sucrose. *[literature, low; novita-0177; [0RHwNzwXG_s 15:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0RHwNzwXG_s&t=909s); 2024-02]*
- For enriched white bread like shokupan, upper limits of about 6% sugar, 10% honey powder, 25% of milk replaced with water, and 5-6% total fat give the best volume, texture and taste. Going above these lowers quality. *[literature, medium; novita-0762; [5dfm-ZiBfMc 11:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5dfm-ZiBfMc&t=717s); 2024-04]*
- Sugar at about 6–8% of flour is the range she recommends for shokupan-style soft bread, based on her experience. *[creator-rule, medium; novita-0352; [UK88XjySuZA 9:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UK88XjySuZA&t=570s); 2025-03]*
- Japanese yudane and tangzhong gelatinize starch so the dough retains water and tastes sweeter. *[literature, medium; novita-0288; [pVzD-Szo-EU 1:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pVzD-Szo-EU&t=69); 2026-02]*
- Honey in the whole wheat dough is optional; she has omitted it with little problem. *[creator-rule, medium; novita-0033; [GZEvf4buPf4 7:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GZEvf4buPf4&t=461s); 2026-08]*
- Sweet mooncake dough for the viral version needed butter and sugar added to a bread-and-milk base; the creator reports no measured sugar amount. *[anecdote, low; novita-0417; [QQkKY7h_SjI 2:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QQkKY7h_SjI&t=150s); 2026-09]*

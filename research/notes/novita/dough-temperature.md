# dough-temperature — Novita Listyani / Seraphine Lishe

Paraphrased from the creators' videos (all rights reserved; facts only). Every bullet links to a claim in `data/claims-novita.jsonl` (id, video, timestamp, evidence label). Dates are video publication dates — her methods changed over time.

## Synthesis

- Mixing at a lower temperature (about 16–17 °C) strengthens gluten: more hydrogen bonds and macro-aggregates, +21 % elasticity at 16 °C, best loaf volume at 17 °C versus 23 and 30 °C. Above about 27 °C dough turns sticky.
- Cold doughs take a little more water and need longer mixing. Use water much colder than the flour (cold water + warmer flour gave the best network after a full ~9 min mix).
- Her tools: ice water, and tangzhong and poolish straight from the fridge.
- **Desired dough temperature formula (mantou video):** water temp = 3 × DDT − flour temp − room temp − friction.

## Claims (11)


### dough-temperature

- Fermenting the poolish first gives the gluten time to form before the final dough is mixed. *[anecdote, low; novita-0286; [e-muI4j42F8 1:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=e-muI4j42F8&t=94); 2020-05]*
- Desired dough temperature sets the water temperature: water = 3 x desired dough temperature - flour temperature - room temperature - friction temperature. In hot, humid conditions the water is iced. *[creator-rule, medium; novita-0773; [YR2e3Kka0TA 3:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YR2e3Kka0TA&t=182s); 2021-05]*
- Ice water lowers the final dough temperature to slow fermentation in a warm kitchen, avoiding over-fermentation and flat loaves. *[creator-rule, medium; novita-0847; [t--K8le97fc 5:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=t--K8le97fc&t=306s); 2022-05]*
- Refrigerating the whole dough slows yeast activity, so fermentation is extended while the dough is still fermenting slowly. *[creator-rule, high; novita-0275; [Evt_8_sl29o 9:07-9:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Evt_8_sl29o&t=547); 2022-05]*
- Tangzhong at 65 degrees C is the target temperature when cooking the scald; the scald is stopped at that point. *[creator-rule, medium; novita-0510; [Wz0PiKOWY1g 5:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Wz0PiKOWY1g&t=327s); 2023-01]*
- Cold tangzhong and poolish are added to the dough on purpose, to bring the final dough temperature down in a warm room; in a colder room the same ingredients may warm the dough. *[creator-rule, medium; novita-0493; [QuhM5qSQ7T0 8:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QuhM5qSQ7T0&t=504s); 2023-11]*
- Cold or ice water is not only about slowing yeast (desired dough temperature) or prepping for cold fermentation; mixing at a lower temperature strengthens the gluten network itself. *[literature, high; novita-0062; [FBKZzNnnRKQ 2:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FBKZzNnnRKQ&t=175s); 2025-09 — cites doi:10.1007/s00217-003-0717-0, doi:10.1016/j.ijrefrig.2020.04.006, ref:parmentier-1778]*
- Doughs mixed at lower temperatures gave better texture and volume (2003 study); lowering mixing temperature with CO2 snow also improved volume. *[literature, high; novita-0063; [FBKZzNnnRKQ 4:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FBKZzNnnRKQ&t=279s); 2025-09 — cites doi:10.1007/s00217-003-0717-0, doi:10.1016/j.ijrefrig.2020.04.006]*
- Dough becomes sticky and less stable above about 27 °C; one study found mixing at 16 °C increased dough elasticity by 21%. *[literature, medium; novita-0065; [FBKZzNnnRKQ 7:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FBKZzNnnRKQ&t=420s); 2025-09 — cites doi:10.1007/s00217-003-0717-0, doi:10.1111/j.1365-2621.2008.01758.x]*
- At equal final dough temperature, hot water + cold flour gave a stronger dough after 3 min of mixing, but after ~9 min cold water + warmer flour gave the strongest, most continuous gluten network (noodle-dough study); aim for water much colder than the flour and allow full mixing time. *[literature, medium; novita-0069; [FBKZzNnnRKQ 9:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FBKZzNnnRKQ&t=564s); 2025-09 — cites doi:10.1016/j.foodres.2025.116209]*
- Using cold refrigerated tangzhong lowers the mixed dough temperature, which she finds improves gluten strength and final volume; aging it too long in the fridge degrades volume. *[creator-rule, medium; novita-0008; [ocBV6-O5SL4 11:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ocBV6-O5SL4&t=705s); 2026-10]*

# flour — Novita Listyani / Seraphine Lishe

Paraphrased from the creators' videos (all rights reserved; facts only). Every bullet links to a claim in `data/claims-novita.jsonl` (id, video, timestamp, evidence label). Dates are video publication dates — her methods changed over time.

## Claims (29)


### flour

- For long-fermented focaccia use a high-protein bread flour, not all-purpose flour, because the dough ferments overnight. *[creator-rule, medium; novita-0220; [0M8A_szL2kI 0:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0M8A_szL2kI&t=40s); 2020-04]*
- A mix of bread flour and all-purpose flour (90 g and 30 g) gives youtiao that are crisp outside and chewy inside. *[anecdote, medium; novita-0797; [p5pLU6y0CpE 1:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=p5pLU6y0CpE&t=107s); 2020-06]*
- High-protein bread flour (14 to 15%) forms more gluten chains than all-purpose flour (11 to 12%) when mixed with water. *[creator-rule, high; novita-0279; [IgaR5XjdOkQ 0:44-1:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IgaR5XjdOkQ&t=44); 2020-11]*
- Flour with ash at or below 0.50% suits white mantou. Lower ash means fewer bran specks and whiter steamed buns. She uses flour with ash below 0.47% and protein of 10.5-11.5%. *[creator-rule, medium; novita-0772; [YR2e3Kka0TA 2:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YR2e3Kka0TA&t=143s); 2021-05]*
- Thin noodles need a higher-protein flour so the dough is elastic enough to stretch; thicker noodles like udon use lower protein. Stronger dough gives firmer, chewier noodles, which suits thin noodles but can be too tough for thick ones. *[creator-rule, medium; novita-0679; [cKSRuyBlpjQ 3:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cKSRuyBlpjQ&t=218s); 2021-10]*
- Bread flour at about 13% protein is a good middle ground for a soft Asian loaf: lower protein (all-purpose) gives a denser loaf; too much protein gives an artisan-like, too-strong crumb. *[creator-rule, medium; novita-0845; [t--K8le97fc 1:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=t--K8le97fc&t=83s); 2022-05]*
- High-protein bread flour gives cinnamon-roll dough the strength to rise well. *[creator-rule, medium; novita-0863; [rvWSBsQLZXM 1:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rvWSBsQLZXM&t=88s); 2022-07]*
- Tapioca starch in the flour blend gives a chewy texture in mochi-style donuts. *[creator-rule, medium; novita-0271; [5UWvfmis7Vs description](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5UWvfmis7Vs); 2022-09]*
- Wheat kernel is about 14% bran, 83% endosperm and 2.5% germ. *[creator-rule, medium; novita-0631; [uNoxI1-jA88 3:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uNoxI1-jA88&t=186s); 2022-09]*
- Bread flour from hard wheat is about 11–13% protein. *[creator-rule, medium; novita-0632; [uNoxI1-jA88 4:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uNoxI1-jA88&t=270s); 2022-09]*
- Patent flour is typically under about 65% extraction; lower extraction gives whiter, less ash flour. *[creator-rule, medium; novita-0634; [uNoxI1-jA88 6:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uNoxI1-jA88&t=385s); 2022-09]*
- US protein and ash figures are on a 14% moisture basis, while EU figures are on a dry basis, so they are not directly comparable. *[creator-rule, medium; novita-0636; [uNoxI1-jA88 9:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uNoxI1-jA88&t=548s); 2022-09]*
- European flours are typically softer than comparable US flours. *[anecdote, low; novita-0637; [uNoxI1-jA88 11:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uNoxI1-jA88&t=713s); 2022-09]*
- Her flour blend (100 g AP flour at about 11% protein and 300 g bread flour at about 13%) gives roughly 12.5% protein and ash near 0.65%, similar to French T65. *[extrapolation, low; novita-0582; [KLHi11p2VrE 8:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KLHi11p2VrE&t=488s); 2022-11]*
- Wheat flour is roughly 63-72% starch (14% moisture basis, varying with protein), 7-15% protein, 2-3% cell-wall arabinoxylans and 2-3% lipids. *[anecdote, low; novita-0640; [hc8AiEc63bM 8:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hc8AiEc63bM&t=483s); 2023-03]*
- A lower-protein flour (all-purpose) gives a lighter, less chewy youtiao than bread flour. *[creator-rule, medium; novita-0267; [-ajZk1KPwO0 8:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-ajZk1KPwO0&t=506s); 2023-07]*
- Use bread flour with about 12 to 13% protein for this donut dough. *[creator-rule, medium; novita-0256; [rvodYO_nuao description](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rvodYO_nuao); 2023-07]*
- Bread flour for this baguette has about 12% protein; total dough protein is about 9% of dough weight. *[creator-rule, medium; novita-0380; [KSGaNRZArBo description](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KSGaNRZArBo); 2023-08]*
- Strong, high-protein bread flour is unsuitable for steamed buns: it overextends in proof and tightens during steaming, giving shrinkage, wrinkles or dimples. *[literature, medium; novita-0805; [bdjvU_fpgok 7:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bdjvU_fpgok&t=432s); 2023-12]*
- Cake flour is not a good substitute for steamed bread: it gives weak elasticity and chewiness and an overly white colour. *[creator-rule, medium; novita-0806; [bdjvU_fpgok 8:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bdjvU_fpgok&t=503s); 2023-12]*
- All-purpose flour gives a slightly yellow mantou; to imitate low-protein bao flour, replace 10-20% of the all-purpose flour with cornstarch. *[creator-rule, medium; novita-0807; [bdjvU_fpgok 11:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bdjvU_fpgok&t=678s); 2023-12]*
- Use a low-protein cake flour for cookies, so that little gluten forms during mixing. *[creator-rule, medium; novita-0694; [qPNc-28unnA 5:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qPNc-28unnA&t=351s); 2024-06]*
- French flours are classified by ash (T number) and strength by alveograph W; W averaged ~90 around Paris (≤70 in provinces) before ~1935–40, whereas modern T55 for baguettes is medium strength, W 160–250. Bean (soy/fava) flour was added as a lipoxygenase source to strengthen weak flour (H2O2 promotes gluten disulfide bonds) and as an amylase carrier. *[literature, high; novita-0104; [8ICSP9xq9o0 5:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8ICSP9xq9o0&t=350s); 2025-04 — cites doi:10.1007/978-1-4757-6809-1]*
- Medium-strong flour gives a more uniform bubble distribution than weak flour. *[literature, low; novita-0565; [dtBqv-qIFLo 8:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dtBqv-qIFLo&t=529s); 2025-05]*
- At low mixing temperatures, soft-wheat flour dough began to behave like hard-wheat flour dough, so cold mixing helps weaker flours most. *[literature, medium; novita-0068; [FBKZzNnnRKQ 8:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FBKZzNnnRKQ&t=491s); 2025-09 — cites doi:10.1094/cchem-06-15-0123-r]*
- Good bread-making flours keep strain hardening up to about 60 °C during baking, whereas poor ones lose it at 45–50 °C, causing earlier bubble coalescence and lower loaf volume. *[literature, high; novita-0045; [7bwsLYvd6go 9:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7bwsLYvd6go&t=562s); 2025-11 — cites doi:10.1016/j.jcs.2007.08.010]*
- Blending all-purpose with bread flour lowers the dough's protein content, which reduces crust water-holding capacity for a crispier, longer-lasting crust. *[creator-rule, medium; novita-0568; [49JXxrvfR3Q 5:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=49JXxrvfR3Q&t=323s); 2026-02]*

### flour.protein

- Higher-protein flours give crusts with higher initial moisture; a looser protein structure (protease treatment) is reported to raise crispness retention several-fold. *[literature, low; novita-0567; [49JXxrvfR3Q 5:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=49JXxrvfR3Q&t=323s); 2026-02]*

### flour.whole-grain

- The main cause of dense 100% whole wheat bread is using a low-protein whole wheat flour; use one with about ≥13.5% protein so the dough can build strength. *[creator-rule, medium; novita-0032; [GZEvf4buPf4 8:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GZEvf4buPf4&t=527s); 2026-08]*

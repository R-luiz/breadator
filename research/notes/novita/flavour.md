# flavour — Novita Listyani / Seraphine Lishe

Paraphrased from the creators' videos (all rights reserved; facts only). Every bullet links to a claim in `data/claims-novita.jsonl` (id, video, timestamp, evidence label). Dates are video publication dates — her methods changed over time.

## Claims (23)


### flavour

- A dough with 100 g fruit yeast water and 100 g poolish gives a mild flavour; she describes it as not sour and not sweet. *[anecdote, low; novita-0525; [fBtJ2ckJ9Vc 13:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=fBtJ2ckJ9Vc&t=791s); 2020-10]*
- Whisk cocoa powder into the still-warm melted chocolate and butter to bloom it and bring out chocolate flavour; cool the mixture before adding to the eggs so they do not cook. *[creator-rule, medium; novita-0850; [rvdsfasP0pM 4:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rvdsfasP0pM&t=249s); 2020-11]*
- Sugar coating of fried donuts should be applied within about one minute of leaving the fryer so it sticks. *[creator-rule, medium; novita-0338; [qLfkJvji7FU 17:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qLfkJvji7FU&t=1056s); 2022-07]*
- Sourdough bread origin about 14,400 years ago, citing a study on early bread from the Levant. *[literature, low; novita-0243; [-T__DFw-awI 2:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-T__DFw-awI&t=124s); 2022-12 — cites doi:10.1073/pnas.1801071115]*
- In a steamed-bread study, matcha slowed early volume rise (up to 120 min), then doughs with matcha rose faster from about 150 min; higher matcha lowered total volume and prolonged fermentation. Sensory scores were highest at 4.5-6% matcha, and the authors suggested limiting matcha to about 6%. *[literature, low; novita-0660; [pLdkpbxYSak 7:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pLdkpbxYSak&t=423s); 2023-07]*
- Bubble formation in dough is attributed to nitrogen nucleation rather than yeast alone. *[anecdote, low; novita-0364; [FxgBEvkhnFg 2:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FxgBEvkhnFg&t=147s); 2023-09]*
- Dipping dough in an alkaline solution before baking speeds up Maillard browning and increases protein hydrolysis, which releases amino acids and gives the dark crust of pretzels. *[creator-rule, medium; novita-0154; [wqqDlDgVJJ0 1:29–3:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wqqDlDgVJJ0&t=89s); 2023-09]*
- Isoamyl alcohol, from leucine, is a common off-flavour in fruit yeast or early cultures. *[anecdote, low; novita-0394; [Pt_dS7FDHog 8:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Pt_dS7FDHog&t=499s); 2023-10]*
- Cocoa fermentation involves wild microbes, which is why cocoa and chocolate carry fermentation flavour. *[anecdote, low; novita-0404; [LewGFXECOe4 0:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LewGFXECOe4&t=31s); 2024-01]*
- Fermentation temperature changes the aroma profile of the dough; at 5 °C ester production increases compared with 35 °C. *[literature, medium; novita-0301; [zNTRKWhFOXQ 11:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zNTRKWhFOXQ&t=672s); 2024-03 — cites doi:10.1016/j.foodres.2013.03.011]*
- Ester and aroma formation in cold fermentation at 5 °C is discussed with reference to 2012 and 2016 papers (6% pressed yeast). *[literature, low; novita-0598; [tZKoPwHGcWU 5:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tZKoPwHGcWU&t=316s); 2024-03]*
- Yeast strains differ in flavour production. *[anecdote, low; novita-0627; [mz3FAr2z2pI 21:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mz3FAr2z2pI&t=1273s); 2024-05]*
- Sensory panels in two studies showed liking of garlic bread falls as garlic powder rises above about 1% of flour, with dislike of strong garlic taste; she caps garlic powder at 1% baker's percentage. One Korean panel of 20 students preferred 1% garlic powder slightly more than control. *[literature, low; novita-0668; [VXyp8CVj3hw 5:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VXyp8CVj3hw&t=319s); 2024-06]*
- Allicin forms only when garlic is cut or crushed (alliinase and alliin mix). Heat denatures alliinase, so cooked or dried garlic makes less allicin; garlic powder may yield less allicin than fresh garlic, and some sources quote up to about 50% alliin loss in powder. *[literature, medium; novita-0669; [VXyp8CVj3hw 5:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VXyp8CVj3hw&t=355s); 2024-06]*
- Maillard browning begins at about 140 C, which is why very hot pan or oven surfaces brown food quickly. *[literature, medium; novita-0431; [hF9nH0WnY88 9:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hF9nH0WnY88&t=562s); 2024-06]*
- Salting cabbage draws water out of it; resting briefly and squeezing out the water before filling avoids a soggy filling. *[creator-rule, high; novita-0432; [hF9nH0WnY88 15:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hF9nH0WnY88&t=950s); 2024-06]*
- Some studies show MSG improves the flavour of salt-reduced bread, especially with spices. A 2022 study found no significant difference in liking with or without MSG, and that study was partly funded by an MSG manufacturer. *[literature, medium; novita-0740; [f6suuWjPSF4 10:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=f6suuWjPSF4&t=638s); 2024-08]*
- In a Dutch in-home study, consumers given salt-reduced soy-sauce bread at first liked it less than regular bread for the first 5 days, but from day 6 there was no significant difference. Liking of the soy bread with sweet fillings rose most. *[literature, medium; novita-0741; [f6suuWjPSF4 11:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=f6suuWjPSF4&t=709s); 2024-08 — cites doi:10.1016/j.foodqual.2012.12.001]*
- Crust aroma comes mainly from baking (Maillard) reactions; crumb aroma mainly from yeast metabolism (pleasant esters, malty/honey/buttery notes) and lipid oxidation (often rancid, fatty, green off-notes, e.g. hexanal, heptanal); staling aroma loss involves rising lipid-oxidation compounds. *[literature, high; novita-0074; [CClhjpjcAd8 9:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CClhjpjcAd8&t=546s); 2025-08 — cites doi:10.1094/cchem-06-13-0121-rw, doi:10.1007/bf01202626, doi:10.1007/s10068-010-0081-3]*
- Low fermentation temperatures increase fruity esters (ethyl acetate, ethyl hexanoate, ethyl octanoate), probably via more acid precursors; long cold yeast fermentation may raise ethanol and higher alcohols (3-methylbutanol, phenylethanol). *[literature, medium; novita-0080; [CClhjpjcAd8 16:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CClhjpjcAd8&t=982s); 2025-08 — cites doi:10.1016/j.lwt.2012.08.019, doi:10.3390/molecules26041035]*
- In yeasted bread, long low-temperature fermentation can limit crust Maillard aromas, though longer fermentation raises crust Strecker aldehydes (malty notes) via proteolysis; in sourdough, long cold fermentation promotes both Maillard and LAB-derived aromas. *[literature, medium; novita-0082; [CClhjpjcAd8 20:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CClhjpjcAd8&t=1207s); 2025-08 — cites doi:10.1006/jcrs.1998.0183]*
- Lipid oxidation starts in the first minutes of mixing: yeast respiration and flour lipoxygenase compete for dissolved oxygen, producing aldehydes and ketones that taste stale or rancid. It happens in lean doughs without added fat. *[literature, medium; novita-0714; [F2nGI0vgpCw 20:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=F2nGI0vgpCw&t=1226s); 2026-01 — cites doi:10.1007/s10068-010-0081-3]*
- In a review comparing industrial, artisan and homemade soft breads, industrial bread had fewer fruity esters (e.g. ethyl octanoate), more aldehydes and furans (lipid oxidation), and more Maillard products (e.g. 2,5-dimethylpyrazine). Artisan bread had more esters, consistent with stronger fermentation; homemade bread had fewer Maillard products. *[literature, medium; novita-0715; [F2nGI0vgpCw 19:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=F2nGI0vgpCw&t=1153s); 2026-01 — cites doi:10.3390/foods11101484]*

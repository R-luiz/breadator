# starter — Novita Listyani / Seraphine Lishe

Paraphrased from the creators' videos (all rights reserved; facts only). Every bullet links to a claim in `data/claims-novita.jsonl` (id, video, timestamp, evidence label). Dates are video publication dates — her methods changed over time.

## Synthesis

- Feed 1:1:1 to 1:2:2 (her leaven); the side-by-side dilutions she tested were 1:1:1, 1:2:2, 1:5:5 and 1:11. A starter takes 5–7 days to establish.
- **Ready** = rises 2–3× and passes a float test; pH ≤ 4.5.
- A refrigerated starter is fed weekly and needs 1–2 room-temperature feeds before use.
- A small levain (20 g starter + 40 + 40) that doubles in 5–6 h matches about 100 g of starter.
- A levain fed with tangzhong (rye shokupan) is ready in about 3–4 h at 1.5×.

## Claims (23)


### starter

- Apple yeast water is used at 90% of the flour weight in the dough. *[creator-rule, medium; novita-0639; [3UFvIpEuYx4 description](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3UFvIpEuYx4&t=0s); 2020-02]*
- Sourdough and fruit yeast water contain less yeast than instant yeast and ferment more slowly, while also adding wild cultures. *[creator-rule, medium; novita-0274; [Evt_8_sl29o 8:31-9:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Evt_8_sl29o&t=511); 2022-05]*
- A starter is wild yeast and lactic acid bacteria from the flour and environment; it needs clean non-chlorinated water and warm conditions, above about 25 °C, to get going. *[creator-rule, medium; novita-0206; [wVZ107LrDMc 0:34–1:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wVZ107LrDMc&t=34s); 2022-06]*
- Acidic additives (yogurt or honey water) lower the early pH and help ward off unwanted bacteria such as Serratia marcescens (red or pink colouring). Once the starter is mature, its own acid and alcohol protect it. *[creator-rule, medium; novita-0207; [wVZ107LrDMc 2:35–3:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wVZ107LrDMc&t=155s); 2022-06]*
- Feeding ratio: 1 part starter : 1 part flour : 1 part water by weight, giving a pourable, poolish-like starter; the flour and water weight equals the starter weight. *[creator-rule, high; novita-0208; [wVZ107LrDMc 6:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wVZ107LrDMc&t=385s); 2022-06]*
- Feed every 12–24 hours at room temperature, once the starter has risen and started to fall; very bubbly and slightly acidic when ready for feeding. *[creator-rule, high; novita-0209; [wVZ107LrDMc 4:32–5:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wVZ107LrDMc&t=272s); 2022-06]*
- Discard part of the starter before each feed to control its size and acidity; if you do not, the yeast and bacteria exceed their food supply. *[creator-rule, high; novita-0210; [wVZ107LrDMc 5:50–6:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wVZ107LrDMc&t=350s); 2022-06]*
- A mature starter rises consistently to 2–3 times its height and smells consistent; check readiness with a float test (a spoonful floats when there is enough gas). *[creator-rule, high; novita-0211; [wVZ107LrDMc 8:26–9:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wVZ107LrDMc&t=506s); 2022-06]*
- Refrigerated starter can be fed about once a week; before using it in bread, let it go through 1–2 room-temperature feeds to regain full activity. *[creator-rule, high; novita-0212; [wVZ107LrDMc 9:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wVZ107LrDMc&t=585s); 2022-06]*
- The longer a starter goes since its last feed, the more acidic it tastes; time the addition to the dough to get the acidity you want. *[creator-rule, high; novita-0213; [wVZ107LrDMc 9:04–9:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wVZ107LrDMc&t=544s); 2022-06]*
- She compared starter dilutions side by side (1:1:1, 1:2:2, 1:5:5, 1:11) and uses 1:2:2 (starter:water:flour) for the leaven. *[measured, medium; novita-0232; [-T__DFw-awI 13:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-T__DFw-awI&t=782s); 2022-12]*
- A starter needs about 5 to 7 days of feeding before it is mature enough to leaven bread reliably. *[creator-rule, medium; novita-0236; [-T__DFw-awI 9:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-T__DFw-awI&t=597s); 2022-12]*
- A 1:1:1 starter that rises to twice its volume by about 5 hours can look ready but may not be at its peak; a 1:5:5 mixture converges with it around 4 to 5 hours and is easily mistaken for it. *[measured, low; novita-0454; [s3eP3SaAFtA 4:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s3eP3SaAFtA&t=273s); 2023-02]*
- Overfeeding a starter, refreshing it two to three times a day at 4 to 5 hour intervals, can make it perform like a much more diluted starter; a floating test on a top sample can pass when the starter is not ready. *[anecdote, low; novita-0455; [s3eP3SaAFtA 5:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s3eP3SaAFtA&t=346s); 2023-02]*
- Bubble size is a readiness clue: big bubbles indicate a starter that has built up enough dissolved carbon dioxide to nucleate and expand. *[creator-rule, medium; novita-0456; [s3eP3SaAFtA 10:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s3eP3SaAFtA&t=608s); 2023-02]*
- A levain of about 6 hours at 29 C more than doubles in volume; a cooler room takes longer and a warmer room faster. *[creator-rule, medium; novita-0843; [Xn4Z0ajKwI4 9:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Xn4Z0ajKwI4&t=576s); 2023-02]*
- Her sourdough maintenance ratio is starter:flour:water 1:1:1; her levain is 1:2:2 (starter:flour:water); her final dough is levain:flour:water 1:5:4. Her workflow aims for a pH drop toward about 4. *[creator-rule, high; novita-0643; [hc8AiEc63bM 16:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hc8AiEc63bM&t=973s); 2023-03]*
- Sourdough leavening must be mature and rise on a predictable schedule; a mature starter is needed for reliable gas production in the dough. *[creator-rule, medium; novita-0683; [A-feCciiAcw 1:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=A-feCciiAcw&t=84s); 2023-08]*
- Fruit yeast water starter is built as 100 g flour plus 100 g fruit yeast water fermented 10–11 h at 27–28 °C to peak; the backslop uses 1:1:1 (starter : water : flour). *[creator-rule, medium; novita-0392; [Pt_dS7FDHog 11:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Pt_dS7FDHog&t=716s); 2023-10]*
- A new sourdough starter usually reaches maturity after about 5 to 7 days of regular feeding. *[literature, low; novita-0730; [qpE_Hz0fJTM 2:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qpE_Hz0fJTM&t=146s); 2023-10]*
- Kombucha (fermented sweet tea): traditionally black or green tea with sugar and a starter, fermented about 10–21 days at 25–30 °C; time depends on temperature. *[creator-rule, medium; novita-0175; [0RHwNzwXG_s 11:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0RHwNzwXG_s&t=695s); 2024-02]*
- Kombucha inoculum: the studies usually add about 10% w/v of liquid plus the SCOBY-housing layer; tea is typically 0.15–1.5% w/v, and substrate must be cooled before adding the starter. *[literature, medium; novita-0179; [0RHwNzwXG_s 14:32–16:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0RHwNzwXG_s&t=872s); 2024-02]*
- A levain built with tangzhong is ready after about 3 hours at room temperature, when it has risen about 1.5 times. *[creator-rule, medium; novita-0321; [qhvJ22U5SBI 8:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qhvJ22U5SBI&t=484s); 2024-09]*

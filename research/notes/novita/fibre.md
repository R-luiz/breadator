# fibre — Novita Listyani / Seraphine Lishe

Paraphrased from the creators' videos (all rights reserved; facts only). Every bullet links to a claim in `data/claims-novita.jsonl` (id, video, timestamp, evidence label). Dates are video publication dates — her methods changed over time.

## Claims (8)


### fibre

- The apple-yeast-water bread is made with no kneading and only three ingredients. *[anecdote, low; novita-0638; [3UFvIpEuYx4 0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3UFvIpEuYx4&t=0s); 2020-02]*
- Bran from whole wheat disrupts the gluten network and reduces loaf volume; sifting the flour is optional. *[creator-rule, medium; novita-0537; [FVE0zv6nam4 1:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FVE0zv6nam4&t=100s); 2023-05]*
- Whole wheat tangzhong soaked overnight reduces phytic acid, improving mineral availability. *[creator-rule, low; novita-0541; [FVE0zv6nam4 2:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FVE0zv6nam4&t=175s); 2023-05]*
- Water-extractable arabinoxylan binds about 15 to 20 times its weight in water. *[literature, low; novita-0506; [Pf5HSzBK6zo 18:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Pf5HSzBK6zo&t=1132s); 2024-01]*
- Spent coffee grounds at 10% of flour weight (about 11% baker's percentage) gave the best aroma and hardness in one shortbread study; in a cookie study 10-20% of flour gave acceptable sensory scores, while 25% increased hardness and reduced acceptability. Cookies with at least 10% grounds were classed as high fibre. *[literature, medium; novita-0692; [qPNc-28unnA 3:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qPNc-28unnA&t=212s); 2024-06 — cites doi:10.1088/1755-1315/947/1/012044]*
- Spent coffee grounds should be dried thoroughly, then finely ground and sieved; coarse grounds make cookies gritty. *[creator-rule, medium; novita-0695; [qPNc-28unnA 5:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qPNc-28unnA&t=317s); 2024-06]*
- Coffee grounds at about 13% of flour, dried before use, are added to the dough. *[creator-rule, medium; novita-0613; [N5otswYiP9U 3:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=N5otswYiP9U&t=186s); 2024-07]*
- Fermented scalds convert water-unextractable arabinoxylans in rye, changing dough and crumb. *[literature, medium; novita-0318; [qhvJ22U5SBI 3:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qhvJ22U5SBI&t=205s); 2024-09 — cites doi:10.3390/foods12244475]*

# sourdough-microbiology — Novita Listyani / Seraphine Lishe

Paraphrased from the creators' videos (all rights reserved; facts only). Every bullet links to a claim in `data/claims-novita.jsonl` (id, video, timestamp, evidence label). Dates are video publication dates — her methods changed over time.

## Claims (32)


### sourdough-microbiology

- Fruit yeast water is not generally acidic, even after long fermentation, with the timeframes she uses. *[anecdote, low; novita-0282; [IgaR5XjdOkQ 7:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IgaR5XjdOkQ&t=464); 2020-11]*
- Below about 30 C the starter favours obligate heterofermentative lactic acid bacteria over other cultures. *[literature, medium; novita-0233; [-T__DFw-awI 6:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-T__DFw-awI&t=416s); 2022-12 — cites doi:10.3390/microorganisms9071355, doi:10.1128/aem.02955-13]*
- Lactic acid bacteria outnumber yeast in sourdough by roughly 100 to 1. *[literature, low; novita-0234; [-T__DFw-awI 11:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-T__DFw-awI&t=671s); 2022-12 — cites doi:10.1128/aem.00572-12]*
- Lactic acid bacteria produce exopolysaccharides that affect sourdough texture and water retention. *[literature, medium; novita-0235; [-T__DFw-awI 7:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-T__DFw-awI&t=454s); 2022-12 — cites doi:10.3390/foods10030635]*
- Heterofermentative lactic acid bacteria in sourdough produce lactic acid plus carbon dioxide, acetic acid, ethanol and other byproducts, which add flavour. *[literature, medium; novita-0457; [s3eP3SaAFtA 13:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s3eP3SaAFtA&t=796s); 2023-02 — cites doi:10.3390/microorganisms9071355]*
- Sourdough fermentation: the levain build gives yeast and bacteria time to balance, which is why she waits 8 h or overnight for the mixture, and a 1:2:2 ratio is used. *[creator-rule, low; novita-0387; [8LCSfWbAnbo 11:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8LCSfWbAnbo&t=668s); 2023-07]*
- In a Japanese sensory study, sourdough bread preferences peaked at about 60–120 min of fermentation and were low at 300 min; ethanol made up about 80% of volatile flavour compounds. *[literature, low; novita-0388; [8LCSfWbAnbo 5:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8LCSfWbAnbo&t=332s); 2023-07]*
- A Belgian study of 127 yeast isolates from 21 sourdoughs in 11 bakeries found baker's yeast in bakery sourdoughs but not in lab-made ones. *[literature, medium; novita-0389; [8LCSfWbAnbo 13:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8LCSfWbAnbo&t=783s); 2023-07]*
- In a French study, baker's yeast was dominant in only about 24% of sourdoughs. *[literature, low; novita-0390; [8LCSfWbAnbo 15:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8LCSfWbAnbo&t=929s); 2023-07]*
- Levain and sourdough baguettes are slow-fermented with a no-knead coil fold method; a long levain build (5–6 h at 28 °C) is used before the dough is mixed. *[creator-rule, high; novita-0375; [KSGaNRZArBo 9:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KSGaNRZArBo&t=566s); 2023-08]*
- Long, cool-ish fermentation tastes better in sourdough than a short one. *[anecdote, low; novita-0379; [KSGaNRZArBo 3:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KSGaNRZArBo&t=180s); 2023-08]*
- Using starter directly in place of a levain would need about 100 g of starter for 400 g flour at 100% hydration, using a 1:4:4 starter:flour:water dilution that gives about three doubling generations. That is five times the 20 g used for the levain. *[extrapolation, medium; novita-0728; [qpE_Hz0fJTM 24:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qpE_Hz0fJTM&t=1464s); 2023-10]*
- Phytase works best near pH 5.0; lactic acid bacteria acidify the dough so phytic acid breaks down; one study reports about 70% phytate breakdown at pH 5.5. *[literature, low; novita-0503; [Pf5HSzBK6zo 10:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Pf5HSzBK6zo&t=632s); 2024-01]*
- Lower pH from lactic acid bacteria is linked to slower digestion of starch, but the effect depends on pH and on proteolysis; the creator flags this as uncertain. *[literature, low; novita-0505; [Pf5HSzBK6zo 16:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Pf5HSzBK6zo&t=991s); 2024-01]*
- Pure sourdough takes much longer than hybrid dough with added instant yeast; a hybrid is the practical route for a sandwich loaf. *[creator-rule, medium; novita-0509; [Pf5HSzBK6zo 26:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Pf5HSzBK6zo&t=1605s); 2024-01]*
- Sourdough made with selected lactic acid bacteria reduced acrylamide in wheat bread by 29.5 to 67.2 percent; chemically acidifying dough with lactic acid did not reduce it. *[literature, low; novita-0453; [jZMwJlzUvHE 6:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jZMwJlzUvHE&t=386s); 2024-02]*
- Doughs held in the 5 to 57 °C range (danger zone) for too long can allow pathogen growth; she cites a 4 to 6 hour limit from the FDA Food Code. *[literature, medium; novita-0296; [zNTRKWhFOXQ 14:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zNTRKWhFOXQ&t=888s); 2024-03]*
- A dough or ferment pH below 4.6 inhibits most foodborne bacteria. *[literature, medium; novita-0297; [zNTRKWhFOXQ 17:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zNTRKWhFOXQ&t=1064s); 2024-03]*
- Salmonella enterica and Staphylococcus aureus can grow in long-fermented no-knead dough. *[literature, medium; novita-0298; [zNTRKWhFOXQ 13:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zNTRKWhFOXQ&t=813s); 2024-03 — cites doi:10.4315/0362-028x.jfp-10-319]*
- In a sourdough study, levain held at 6 °C had glucose about 1.5 times higher after long fermentation. *[literature, low; novita-0600; [tZKoPwHGcWU 14:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tZKoPwHGcWU&t=845s); 2024-03]*
- Type I (back-slopped) household sourdough: about 20–30% of the flour is pre-fermented for more than 6 h, and the proofing time generally exceeds 2 h. Sourdough is usually the sole leavening. *[literature, medium; novita-0147; [bIP-8B2P1A8 35:09–35:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bIP-8B2P1A8&t=2109s); 2024-05]*
- In type I sourdough (pH below 4.5) flour endogenous proteases and lactic acid bacteria proteases cause significant proteolysis; free amino acids rise almost linearly with fermentation time, which contributes to flavour. *[literature, medium; novita-0148; [bIP-8B2P1A8 36:53–37:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bIP-8B2P1A8&t=2213s); 2024-05]*
- Phytate degradation is not unique to sourdough: moderate acidification of dough to about pH 5.5 with lactic acid, with no lactic acid bacteria, gave phytate degradation similar to sourdough fermentation. Lower phytate in vitro does not automatically mean better mineral status in people. *[literature, medium; novita-0152; [bIP-8B2P1A8 45:21–46:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bIP-8B2P1A8&t=2721s); 2024-05]*
- Salt has different effects on the two sourdough microbes: up to about 0.7% salt stimulates lactic acid bacteria, while higher levels suppress them much more strongly than yeast. Build a levain before adding a starter to a salted dough. *[literature, medium; novita-0736; [f6suuWjPSF4 5:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=f6suuWjPSF4&t=353s); 2024-08 — cites doi:10.1016/s0740-0020(02)00117-x]*
- The levain for this shokupan ferments 3 to 4 h at room temperature until it reaches about 1.5 times its volume. *[creator-rule, medium; novita-0248; [tyoILEdRiK8 description](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tyoILEdRiK8); 2024-08]*
- Cold slows acid production rate in sourdough; cold-fermented sourdough is more acidic only because the longer total time accumulates more acid. *[literature, medium; novita-0081; [CClhjpjcAd8 18:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CClhjpjcAd8&t=1132s); 2025-08 — cites doi:10.1007/978-3-031-23084-4, doi:10.1016/j.fbio.2020.100704]*
- LAB outnumber yeast in a typical starter by up to 100:1; one study found adding salt shifted the LAB:yeast ratio from about 20:1 to 1:1 or lower, because LAB are much less salt tolerant than yeast. *[literature, medium; novita-0055; [doZTKqN8Lls 9:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=doZTKqN8Lls&t=545s); 2025-10 — cites doi:10.1016/s0740-0020(02)00117-x, doi:10.1016/bs.aambs.2017.02.003]*
- To make sourdough milder, add salt to the levain (e.g. 2% baker's percentage as in the Monheim process): fewer LAB means less acid. *[creator-rule, medium; novita-0057; [doZTKqN8Lls 11:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=doZTKqN8Lls&t=684s); 2025-10 — cites doi:10.1016/s0740-0020(02)00117-x]*
- To make sourdough more sour, add a small amount of salt to the levain: low salt (up to about 0.7% of the levain/starter) slightly stimulates LAB, raising lactic and acetic acid; TTA is minimized at high salt. *[literature, medium; novita-0058; [doZTKqN8Lls 11:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=doZTKqN8Lls&t=718s); 2025-10 — cites doi:10.1016/s0740-0020(02)00117-x]*
- Cold-fermented sourdough tastes more acidic mainly because fermentation is longer (many LAB prefer warmth for peak acid); the optimal salt level for LAB growth may rise as temperature falls, one study finding 1.6% salt optimal at 15 °C. *[literature, medium; novita-0059; [doZTKqN8Lls 13:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=doZTKqN8Lls&t=795s); 2025-10 — cites doi:10.1016/s0740-0020(02)00117-x]*
- Salt tolerance differs by species: Fructilactobacillus sanfranciscensis is inhibited by 4% salt while Lactiplantibacillus plantarum tolerates up to 6%, so high salt selects for salt-tolerant species; starters vary widely (a 500-starter study), so responses to salt vary. *[literature, medium; novita-0060; [doZTKqN8Lls 15:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=doZTKqN8Lls&t=904s); 2025-10 — cites doi:10.7554/elife.61644, doi:10.1016/bs.aambs.2017.02.003]*
- Lactic acid bacteria in a starter can outnumber yeast by up to 100 to 1 without salt; yeast tolerates the osmotic stress of salt much better than the bacteria do. *[literature, low; novita-0531; [T3qc-pMrIk4 0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=T3qc-pMrIk4&t=0s); 2026-05]*

# troubleshooting — Novita Listyani / Seraphine Lishe

Paraphrased from the creators' videos (all rights reserved; facts only). Every bullet links to a claim in `data/claims-novita.jsonl` (id, video, timestamp, evidence label). Dates are video publication dates — her methods changed over time.

## Claims (11)


### troubleshooting

- The target choux consistency is checked by a V shape when lifted with a spatula. *[creator-rule, high; novita-0606; [9OX9XNzoNXI 5:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9OX9XNzoNXI&t=349s); 2020-10]*
- Yellowing comes from high dough pH, such as from baking powder or soda, which activates flour xanthophyll pigments. Hard water and clumps of leavener cause yellow spots. Unpigmented white mantou needs neither. *[creator-rule, medium; novita-0774; [YR2e3Kka0TA 8:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YR2e3Kka0TA&t=485s); 2021-05]*
- Gummy mantou comes from damp flour, over-long fermentation (including starting buns in a cold steamer that warms and over-ferments), too low steam, and too short steaming. *[creator-rule, medium; novita-0780; [YR2e3Kka0TA 14:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YR2e3Kka0TA&t=861s); 2021-05]*
- Noodles are boiled in small batches for about 1-1.5 min with stirring; undercooked noodles taste soggy. Tossing them in oil right after cooking prevents sticking. *[creator-rule, medium; novita-0682; [cKSRuyBlpjQ 13:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cKSRuyBlpjQ&t=811s); 2021-10]*
- Use aluminium-free baking powder for youtiao. *[creator-rule, low; novita-0269; [-ajZk1KPwO0 4:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-ajZk1KPwO0&t=295s); 2023-07]*
- Pita puffs only if the internal temperature reaches at least 100 C, so the water turns to steam and the bread is fully baked. *[creator-rule, medium; novita-0814; [CIBNQUs62Lg 1:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CIBNQUs62Lg&t=73s); 2023-09]*
- Target pH of a mature sourdough starter is 4.5 or lower; the creator uses this as the sign it is ready. *[creator-rule, medium; novita-0393; [Pt_dS7FDHog 5:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Pt_dS7FDHog&t=321s); 2023-10]*
- Chlorinated tap water should be boiled 15 min or left to stand 24 h before use in cultures. *[creator-rule, medium; novita-0395; [Pt_dS7FDHog 3:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Pt_dS7FDHog&t=180s); 2023-10]*
- Target a dough pH of about 6.4-6.7 for mantou. Over-acid dough gives a grey, small, dense, sour bun; too much alkali gives yellow colour and an alkali smell. *[creator-rule, medium; novita-0811; [bdjvU_fpgok 17:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bdjvU_fpgok&t=1066s); 2023-12]*
- Blisters on steamed bread come from air bubbles trapped during mixing that were not removed by degassing; they appear at the surface in the first minutes of steaming. *[creator-rule, medium; novita-0813; [bdjvU_fpgok 27:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bdjvU_fpgok&t=1667s); 2023-12]*
- Over-fermented dough gets sticky because proteolysis and acids release previously bound water (more free water). *[creator-rule, medium; novita-0101; [PEoTSDswa58 14:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PEoTSDswa58&t=849s); 2025-04]*

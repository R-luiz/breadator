# yeast — Novita Listyani / Seraphine Lishe

Paraphrased from the creators' videos (all rights reserved; facts only). Every bullet links to a claim in `data/claims-novita.jsonl` (id, video, timestamp, evidence label). Dates are video publication dates — her methods changed over time.

## Synthesis

- Never rehydrate instant yeast in water; whisk it into flour.
- **Conversions:** 6 % fresh ≈ 2 % instant (≈3:1). Fresh yeast is 60–75 % moisture.
- **Yeast levels she uses:** 0.2 % of total flour in the poolish of overnight breads; about 1 % in straight enriched doughs (0.7 % when rests are long); 2 % for a 3 h cold ferment at 5 °C.
- She argues against tiny-yeast long cold ferments: food safety and more lipid oxidation. In 2022, though, she said long flavour ferments need "very little yeast".
- **Salt-stressed yeast:** 7 % salt solution for 40 min before sweet doughs. **Osmotolerant yeast:** for sugar above about 16 % (or add about 1 g more yeast).
- **Hybrid sourdough:** about 0.2 % instant yeast added to a weak levain dough.

- Long fermentation for flavour needs very little yeast; the goal is volatile-compound development, not gas production, so excessive yeast is unnecessary. *[creator-rule, high; novita-0577; [KLHi11p2VrE 2:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KLHi11p2VrE&t=137s); 2022-11]*

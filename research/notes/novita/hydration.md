# hydration — Novita Listyani / Seraphine Lishe

Paraphrased from the creators' videos (all rights reserved; facts only). Every bullet links to a claim in `data/claims-novita.jsonl` (id, video, timestamp, evidence label). Dates are video publication dates — her methods changed over time.

## Synthesis

- Count tangzhong and poolish water, and milk at ×0.87 (egg ×0.75). BakingSmart also counts butter at about 16 % and honey at about 17 %, which raises her quoted hydration by 1–2.5 points.
- Tangzhong raises optimal hydration by about 1.5–3 points per 10 % of flour scalded (see scalds).
- Water mobility: kneading turns free water into bound water, which is why dough stops being sticky. Over-fermentation releases bound water and the dough gets sticky again.

## Claims (44)


### hydration

- Add the water to the poolish gradually, a third at a time, alternating with flour; adding all the water at once makes the dough too wet to handle. *[creator-rule, high; novita-0215; [7LuO4wfL-uI 3:19–4:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7LuO4wfL-uI&t=199s); 2020-04]*
- About 73% hydration gives big open holes in the crumb. *[creator-rule, medium; novita-0201; [wl7zl__jSGI 2:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wl7zl__jSGI&t=136s); 2020-09]*
- A pita puffs into a pocket when the dough is wet enough to make steam, has strong gluten, has no cracks or tears, is rolled thin, and is baked in a very hot pan. *[creator-rule, medium; novita-0445; [XwJrLKXz3Cg 1:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XwJrLKXz3Cg&t=103s); 2020-12]*
- A 75% hydration no-knead loaf uses 200 g bread flour with 150 g fruit yeast water, mixed only until homogeneous; the dough stays shaggy and tears when stretched, which is intended. *[creator-rule, medium; novita-0709; [mzAXbTCWUOE 2:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mzAXbTCWUOE&t=124s); 2021-03]*
- 100% hydration dough (equal weights flour and water): the goal is to move from a slack dough to a workable one through repeated stretch and folds with rests (about 30 min between sets), then lamination in early bulk and three coil folds. *[creator-rule, medium; novita-0224; [4Cpsgir28HM 1:12–6:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4Cpsgir28HM&t=72s); 2021-03]*
- Noodle dough at about 40% hydration (water to flour) is high for noodles and is easier to knead and roll; wetter doughs give softer noodles, drier doughs give more bite. *[creator-rule, medium; novita-0680; [cKSRuyBlpjQ 6:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cKSRuyBlpjQ&t=364s); 2021-10]*
- Soak bran in water for about 3 hours before mixing, so it softens, is easier to digest, and does not cut the gluten; bran is very water hungry. *[creator-rule, medium; novita-0469; [WUilMHvvpCk 1:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WUilMHvvpCk&t=79s); 2022-07]*
- Eggs are mostly water and their size varies, so weigh eggs to know how much water they add. Several eggs can make dough sticky. *[creator-rule, medium; novita-0800; [uxlYDs03HfI 2:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uxlYDs03HfI&t=177s); 2022-08]*
- Lower-hydration doughs (under about 75%) are best handled with straight stretch-and-fold; higher-hydration doughs suit slap-and-fold, coil-fold, lamination or no-knead methods. *[creator-rule, medium; novita-0616; [PusqiSDR090 4:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PusqiSDR090&t=286s); 2022-09]*
- Bread flour absorbs more water than all-purpose flour, so hydration may need to be adjusted. *[creator-rule, medium; novita-0618; [PusqiSDR090 9:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PusqiSDR090&t=567s); 2022-09]*
- Higher-ash flour absorbs more water and needs a longer rest. *[creator-rule, medium; novita-0635; [uNoxI1-jA88 7:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uNoxI1-jA88&t=461s); 2022-09]*
- No extra water is needed when scalding under 10% of the flour; above 10%, add about 2 percentage points of water per +10% of flour scalded (as in the paper), mainly to cover evaporation and water held by the scald. Adding much more water also dilutes a gluten network already reduced by protein denatured in the scald. *[literature, medium; novita-0129; [_mFYeiLzLpo 17:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_mFYeiLzLpo&t=1040s); 2022-10 — cites doi:10.3136/nskkk.62.547]*
- Keep the final dough wet and add flour sparingly during preshaping and shaping: only the outer tense layer is floured, to avoid dense, tough baguettes. *[creator-rule, high; novita-0581; [KLHi11p2VrE 14:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KLHi11p2VrE&t=888s); 2022-11]*
- Raising hydration to about 75% when adding tangzhong is not needed; the scald liquid is already part of the dough's water. The creator says the dough would otherwise become sticky. *[creator-rule, medium; novita-0511; [Wz0PiKOWY1g 10:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Wz0PiKOWY1g&t=608s); 2023-01 — cites doi:10.3136/fstr.20.1071]*
- Farinograph water absorption of a 30 to 40% tangzhong dough rises by less than 2%, so raising the recipe to 75% hydration is far beyond what the dough can take. *[literature, medium; novita-0514; [Wz0PiKOWY1g 10:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Wz0PiKOWY1g&t=648s); 2023-01 — cites doi:10.3136/fstr.20.1071]*
- Treat tangzhong (like levain) as a derivative of flour and water, not a single ingredient: count its flour and water in total flour and hydration, otherwise hydration is miscalculated. *[creator-rule, high; novita-0138; [OpK1CeW9xm8 28:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OpK1CeW9xm8&t=1732s); 2023-01]*
- Dough water is partitioned roughly as ~45% to starch, up to ~25% to proteins and ~25% to arabinoxylans; arabinoxylans can bind up to ~10x their dry weight in water. *[anecdote, low; novita-0641; [hc8AiEc63bM 8:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hc8AiEc63bM&t=520s); 2023-03]*

- Tangzhong 35 bread with about 69% hydration went stale faster than tangzhong 20 bread; at about 73-74% hydration the tangzhong 35 bread stayed softer longer. *[measured, low; novita-0825; [HJ6LMqWrZp8 7:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HJ6LMqWrZp8&t=427s); 2023-05 — cites ref:ke-yuwen-2004-thesis]*
- Hydration of the final dough should be optimal (about 73% for tangzhong 35): enough water so the tangzhong starch hydrates well, without weakening the final dough's gluten. *[literature, medium; novita-0829; [HJ6LMqWrZp8 11:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HJ6LMqWrZp8&t=664s); 2023-05]*
- Whole wheat dough needs more water: she raises hydration from about 70% to 76% for a whole-wheat tangzhong loaf. *[creator-rule, medium; novita-0538; [FVE0zv6nam4 3:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FVE0zv6nam4&t=210s); 2023-05]*

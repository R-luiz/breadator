# proof — Novita Listyani / Seraphine Lishe

Paraphrased from the creators' videos (all rights reserved; facts only). Every bullet links to a claim in `data/claims-novita.jsonl` (id, video, timestamp, evidence label). Dates are video publication dates — her methods changed over time.

## Synthesis

- **Cues:** almost doubled. In pans: 1–2 cm below the rim (2026 XML pans 21×11×12 cm); 3–4 cm below the edge for some shokupan. She prefers slightly under- to over-proofed. Tangzhong doughs need longer proofs, and at very high hydration the poke test misleads.
- **Times at 27–28 °C:** rolls 40–60 min; enriched pan loaves 1–1.5 h (2–3 h for sourdough hybrids); always "longer in cooler rooms".
- **Cold retard:** fridge at 3–5 °C. Quick 2–3 h cold ferment with 2 % instant yeast, or a 1–2 h cold bulk plus 1 h cold proof (artisan XML). Long retards are 8–36 h; beyond about 24 h enriched doughs weaken and sour.
- **Freezing dough:** freeze before fermentation starts and keep it frozen no more than about a month. Help survival with honey at 6–8 %, higher-protein flour, solid fat, slightly more (or osmotolerant/freeze-tolerant) yeast and tangzhong. Avoid freeze–thaw cycles.

### proof.cold-retard

- Cold fermentation = fermenting dough in the fridge at about 3–5 °C (cold proofing = the same for shaped dough). *[creator-rule, high; novita-0072; [CClhjpjcAd8 1:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CClhjpjcAd8&t=106s); 2025-08]*
- Fermentation at 15 °C and 35 °C produced more crumb lipid-oxidation compounds (notably hexanal, heptanal) than at 5 °C; because lipid oxidation is temperature dependent, even a short 2–3 h cold ferment improves flavour by limiting off-flavours. *[literature, high; novita-0075; [CClhjpjcAd8 11:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CClhjpjcAd8&t=688s); 2025-08 — cites doi:10.1016/j.lwt.2012.08.019]*
- Her method evolved: previously she maximized flavour with a preferment, room-temperature bulk and proof and no extra yeast; she now also retards the final dough (with a bit more yeast) as a quick flavour option; room-temperature fermentation gives a bolder crust flavour, cold gives softer fruity notes. *[creator-rule, medium; novita-0084; [CClhjpjcAd8 24:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CClhjpjcAd8&t=1455s); 2025-08]*

### proof.freezing

- Freezing dough kills or damages part of the yeast and dehydrates gluten through water redistribution and ice recrystallization; dead yeast releases glutathione that further weakens gluten; frozen storage also orders starch crystallization. Result: lower volume and harder bread, worse with longer storage and slower freezing (freezer not cold enough). *[literature, medium; novita-0139; [yevjgX3SvlM 0:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yevjgX3SvlM&t=35s); 2024-06]*
- Honey at 6–8% (of flour) protects gluten during freezing and reduces staling; consumers rated bread from frozen dough with honey as favourably as fresh bread. *[literature, medium; novita-0140; [yevjgX3SvlM 3:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yevjgX3SvlM&t=212s); 2024-06]*
- For frozen dough use higher-protein flour and some solid fat: a firmer (lauric-acid-rich) shortening protected the gluten matrix through 2 months of frozen storage while a softer one showed damage after 1 month; she uses butter as a tasty compromise. *[literature, medium; novita-0141; [yevjgX3SvlM 4:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yevjgX3SvlM&t=243s); 2024-06]*
- Partially pre-heated (gelatinized) dough portions improved frozen-dough bread and slowed deterioration by strengthening gluten and holding water (less ice recrystallization); tangzhong/yudane likely helps similarly. *[literature, medium; novita-0142; [yevjgX3SvlM 5:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yevjgX3SvlM&t=329s); 2024-06]*
- Freeze dough before fermentation starts (minimize fermentation before freezing) to improve yeast freeze tolerance; beyond about a month storage problems grow, and avoid freeze–thaw cycles. *[literature, medium; novita-0144; [yevjgX3SvlM 8:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yevjgX3SvlM&t=491s); 2024-06]*
- Doughs with partially pregelatinized flour (tangzhong) may survive freezing better. *[creator-rule, low; novita-0021; [ocBV6-O5SL4 25:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ocBV6-O5SL4&t=1554s); 2026-10]*

### proof.retard

- Youtiao dough can be cold-fermented in the fridge for 16 to 20 h, or kneaded in a mixer and then fermented for about 4 h. Both routes need the dough to pass windowpane. *[creator-rule, medium; novita-0794; [p5pLU6y0CpE 1:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=p5pLU6y0CpE&t=107s); 2020-06]*

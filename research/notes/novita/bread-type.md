# bread-type — Novita Listyani / Seraphine Lishe

Paraphrased from the creators' videos (all rights reserved; facts only). Every bullet links to a claim in `data/claims-novita.jsonl` (id, video, timestamp, evidence label). Dates are video publication dates — her methods changed over time.

## Claims (20)


### bread-type

- Frying oil at 160-175 °C puffs youtiao well. Too cold gives greasy, under-puffed sticks; too hot browns the outside while the inside stays raw. Fry 3-4 min, turning. *[creator-rule, medium; novita-0795; [p5pLU6y0CpE 6:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=p5pLU6y0CpE&t=368s); 2020-06]*
- Her beginner sequence of bread-making steps: preferment, mixing, bulk fermentation, dividing, preshaping, bench rest, shaping, final proofing, baking, cooling. *[creator-rule, high; novita-0869; [0X5U-ZHSM08 description](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0X5U-ZHSM08); 2021-01]*
- Her three rises (bulk, proof, oven spring) are used as cues that map to the stages of a sourdough. *[extrapolation, low; novita-0237; [-T__DFw-awI 21:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-T__DFw-awI&t=1276s); 2022-12]*
- For her pan, the finished dough weight was 540 to 600 g, i.e. about 257 to 286 g of dough per litre of pan volume. Use it as a dough density target relative to pan volume. *[creator-rule, medium; novita-0478; [qdOHmdTTs24 4:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qdOHmdTTs24&t=247s); 2023-04]*
- To scale a baker's-percentage recipe to a pan: multiply pan volume by dough density to get the dough weight needed, then scale the flour weight in proportion and recompute the other ingredients from baker's percentages. *[creator-rule, medium; novita-0230; [b2P9UVxCce8 0:00–0:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=b2P9UVxCce8&t=36s); 2023-05]*
- Youtiao dough with yeast only (no chemical raising agent) is her method; she states this differs from common youtiao recipes that use baking powder. *[creator-rule, medium; novita-0263; [-ajZk1KPwO0 description](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-ajZk1KPwO0); 2023-07]*
- Dinner rolls: a tangzhong at 20% of flour with a poolish and an egg gives softness and sweetness. *[creator-rule, medium; novita-0543; [txzOxC86ePU 0:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=txzOxC86ePU&t=43s); 2023-11]*
- Bake dinner rolls at 180 degrees C top and bottom for 20 min, then bottom heat for 10 min. *[creator-rule, medium; novita-0550; [txzOxC86ePU 20:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=txzOxC86ePU&t=1230s); 2023-11]*
- A 20% rye loaf with the same ingredients shows no volume difference compared with the wheat version. *[literature, low; novita-0247; [tyoILEdRiK8 9:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tyoILEdRiK8&t=572s); 2024-08]*
- Loaf shape changes sensory outcome: a flatter, wider loaf with more surface area bakes faster and tastes less bitter and saltier (more water loss concentrates salt), and gives less lingering aftertaste. *[literature, medium; novita-0656; [gPYzcoOgatI 16:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gPYzcoOgatI&t=1016s); 2025-07 — cites doi:10.1016/j.lwt.2020.110024]*
- The 1993 French Bread Decree defines bread in the French tradition as made only from bread flour, water, salt and yeast or sourdough. It bans additives such as ascorbic acid, lecithin and fava flour, and restricts the term boulangerie to on-site production by qualified bakers. *[literature, medium; novita-0719; [F2nGI0vgpCw 15:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=F2nGI0vgpCw&t=934s); 2026-01 — cites ref:french-decret-pain-1993]*
- Ciabatta has a mild flavour and aroma and lacks the lingering fermentation flavour of other breads. Smaller loaves of ciabatta shape were rated for lower flavour persistency and low bitterness in a 2021 sensory study. *[literature, medium; novita-0190; [3x4p_bECQbM 1:44–2:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3x4p_bECQbM&t=104s); 2026-01 — cites doi:10.1016/j.lwt.2020.110024]*
- Oreo-style fillings add fat and sugar to a bread-based dough; the creator noted the filling was small on the packs she tested. *[anecdote, low; novita-0418; [QQkKY7h_SjI 4:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QQkKY7h_SjI&t=293s); 2026-09]*

### bread-type.enriched

- 100% whole wheat all-purpose dough formula: total flour 375 g; tangzhong 40 g whole wheat flour + 80 g boiling water (~10% of flour, 1:2); poolish 170 g whole wheat + 170 g water + 2 g yeast (45% of flour); final 165 g whole wheat, 20 g honey, 45 g milk, 25 g butter, 6.5 g salt; stated hydration 289 g (~77%). *[creator-rule, high; novita-0034; [DtsBfGiq1H0 description](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DtsBfGiq1H0); 2026-08]*

### bread-type.flatbread

- Pita from the same dough: roll flat rounds, rest 30 min, cook in a pan heated to about 200 °C, pressing down, flipping, until fully puffed, then 1–2 min more on the other side. *[creator-rule, high; novita-0042; [DtsBfGiq1H0 10:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DtsBfGiq1H0&t=620s); 2026-08]*

### bread-type.fried

- Most oil absorption in fried dough happens during the cooling phase after frying, not during frying itself. *[creator-rule, medium; novita-0840; [qXhVxe1DDXE 15:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qXhVxe1DDXE&t=901s); 2023-04]*

### bread-type.pizza

- Traditional Neapolitan-style pizza dough is about 55–59% hydration baked 1–2 min at 300–400 °C; at home she raises hydration to ~70–80%, adds tangzhong 20 and bakes as hot as the oven allows until crisp. *[creator-rule, medium; novita-0136; [OpK1CeW9xm8 19:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OpK1CeW9xm8&t=1168s); 2023-01]*
- Pizza-dough rheology study: dough with 20% gelatinized starch behaved better than a 60%-hydration control even at 80% hydration, so a tangzhong-20 pizza dough at >70% hydration handles like a ~60% control; the modified pizza crust was softer and its cornicione moister and softer in sensory tests even with longer baking. *[literature, high; novita-0137; [OpK1CeW9xm8 26:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OpK1CeW9xm8&t=1609s); 2023-01 — cites doi:10.5458/jag.jag.jag-2021_0014]*
- Tangzhong is especially useful in lean breads baked hot (pizza, baguette): it retains moisture so a crisp crust keeps a soft, flexible crumb in home ovens that cannot reach wood-fired temperatures; it also makes higher hydrations manageable. *[creator-rule, medium; novita-0121; [DjQ7EtvzK_w 21:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DjQ7EtvzK_w&t=1294s); 2023-09]*
- Ice-water mixing should benefit high-hydration pizza doughs most (firmer, easier to handle, possibly airier crust). *[extrapolation, low; novita-0070; [FBKZzNnnRKQ 8:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FBKZzNnnRKQ&t=525s); 2025-09]*

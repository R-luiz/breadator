# fat — Novita Listyani / Seraphine Lishe

Paraphrased from the creators' videos (all rights reserved; facts only). Every bullet links to a claim in `data/claims-novita.jsonl` (id, video, timestamp, evidence label). Dates are video publication dates — her methods changed over time.

## Claims (33)


### fat

- Enclose frozen butter in a small dough wrapper (about 10 g) and pinch the seams tightly, so the butter does not leak out of the bun in the oven. *[creator-rule, medium; novita-0788; [T9SdsTAILj4 5:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=T9SdsTAILj4&t=323s); 2020-07]*
- Melted butter coats flour proteins and helps keep the crumb moist, and adds flavour; melt it fully and let it cool before adding. *[creator-rule, medium; novita-0860; [XotTKXl3NQ4 1:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XotTKXl3NQ4&t=95s); 2020-11]*
- Cold butter added in portions keeps the dough cooler, because mixing a rich dough long enough to develop gluten warms it through friction. *[creator-rule, medium; novita-0462; [kdv4d241PTk 4:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kdv4d241PTk&t=255s); 2020-12]*
- Add butter in portions after the dough is well developed, letting each portion fully incorporate before the next, then mix to windowpane. *[creator-rule, high; novita-0463; [kdv4d241PTk 4:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kdv4d241PTk&t=286s); 2020-12]*
- 10 g olive oil on 200 g flour (5% fat) softens high-hydration bread a little. *[creator-rule, medium; novita-0228; [4Cpsgir28HM 2:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4Cpsgir28HM&t=146s); 2021-03]*
- Total fat including milk fat should be 2–4% of flour; above the upper threshold (about 8 g on 200 g flour) quality drops, so shortening is optional. *[literature, medium; novita-0410; [1Wwvxu8C8pk 1:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1Wwvxu8C8pk&t=71s); 2021-05]*
- Butter goes in after the dough is kneaded, because fat interferes with gluten development; milk powder adds milk richness without changing the liquid balance. *[creator-rule, high; novita-0848; [t--K8le97fc 4:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=t--K8le97fc&t=269s); 2022-05]*
- Add butter after the gluten has formed: fat coats the flour and makes gluten form slowly, so adding it early can give a cake-like texture and a longer knead. *[creator-rule, high; novita-0852; [hUKMgsUGKoU 5:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hUKMgsUGKoU&t=308s); 2022-05]*
- Fat competes with water for flour and coats gluten strands, so gluten forms more slowly and the crumb becomes finer and denser; at sufficient amounts, fat can film gas bubbles and increase volume. *[creator-rule, medium; novita-0853; [hUKMgsUGKoU 1:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hUKMgsUGKoU&t=113s); 2022-05]*
- Solid fats such as shortening are said to improve bread volume more than liquid fats. *[literature, medium; novita-0854; [hUKMgsUGKoU 6:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hUKMgsUGKoU&t=391s); 2022-05]*
- Butter contains some water, so count it in hydration when calculating a formula. *[creator-rule, high; novita-0855; [hUKMgsUGKoU 3:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hUKMgsUGKoU&t=187s); 2022-05]*
- Oil on the bench and bowl prevents sticking and makes low-hydration dough easier to handle without adding flour; butter brushed on as a glaze melts into the crust, adding flavour and softness. *[creator-rule, medium; novita-0856; [hUKMgsUGKoU 7:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hUKMgsUGKoU&t=429s); 2022-05]*
- Add butter after the dough has begun to develop gluten, because fat interferes with gluten development if added at the start. *[creator-rule, high; novita-0470; [WUilMHvvpCk 1:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WUilMHvvpCk&t=115s); 2022-07]*
- Fat interferes with gluten development, so richer doughs call for higher-protein bread flour to support the structure. *[creator-rule, high; novita-0335; [qLfkJvji7FU 0:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qLfkJvji7FU&t=41s); 2022-07]*
- Sweetened condensed milk is about 45% sugar and about 8% fat. *[creator-rule, medium; novita-0608; [B37m_f3qdWE 3:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=B37m_f3qdWE&t=190s); 2022-08]*
- Add butter after gluten has developed; the fat interferes with gluten development if added early. *[creator-rule, medium; novita-0270; [5UWvfmis7Vs 3:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5UWvfmis7Vs&t=238s); 2022-09]*
- Retarding dough in the fridge makes the crust show many small blisters, which the creator attributes to CO2 leaking through the surface. *[anecdote, low; novita-0576; [KLHi11p2VrE 12:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KLHi11p2VrE&t=747s); 2022-11]*
- Whole milk at about 3.5% fat and total fat up to about 5% of flour improve texture and volume in a white loaf and slow staling. *[creator-rule, low; novita-0485; [qdOHmdTTs24 15:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qdOHmdTTs24&t=927s); 2023-04]*
- Total fat of about 6% of flour (including the fat in milk and egg) gives a tender crumb; the whole egg also adds dough strength and stability. *[creator-rule, medium; novita-0486; [qdOHmdTTs24 16:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qdOHmdTTs24&t=962s); 2023-04]*
- About 6% fat (butter as a share of flour) softens the bread and helps it last longer. *[creator-rule, medium; novita-0168; [iPaBk4U33sM 1:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=iPaBk4U33sM&t=66s); 2023-06]*
- Total fat around 8-9% (baker's percent, including butter and milk fat) is at the upper end of the usual soft-bun range; the fat tenderises the bun and, with tangzhong, extends shelf life. *[creator-rule, medium; novita-0822; [zClatNjlIPc 10:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zClatNjlIPc&t=640s); 2023-07]*
- Total fat about 6% of flour is the optimum for texture and volume in this enriched bread (about 9% fat including egg). *[literature, medium; novita-0397; [aLYDjuEHrT0 1:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=aLYDjuEHrT0&t=71s); 2024-01]*
- Allicin is soluble in fat but not well dispersed in water, so fat such as butter spreads garlic flavour through the dough. *[creator-rule, medium; novita-0670; [VXyp8CVj3hw 7:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VXyp8CVj3hw&t=423s); 2024-06]*
- Chilling cookie dough (a log for about 45 min, then shaped pieces for about 1 h) makes it firm enough to shape and keeps it from spreading in the oven; a brief re-chill helps if it gets sticky. *[creator-rule, medium; novita-0696; [qPNc-28unnA 9:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qPNc-28unnA&t=566s); 2024-06]*
- About 5% solid fat (baker's percent) gives the maximum bread volume; beyond that volume barely changes, and too much fat inhibits proof (brioche with over 50% butter is dense). *[literature, medium; novita-0830; [7vLYCrhbgaU 2:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7vLYCrhbgaU&t=179s); 2024-07]*
- Solid fats raise bread volume more than oils: the fat crystals stick to gas-liquid interfaces and stabilise gas cells; oil droplets do not, and oil-based doughs show more holes in the gluten film and less volume. *[literature, medium; novita-0831; [7vLYCrhbgaU 6:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7vLYCrhbgaU&t=395s); 2024-07 — cites doi:10.1111/j.1745-4549.2004.23079.x, doi:10.1006/jcrs.1996.0052]*
- Beta-prime fat crystals (smaller than beta crystals) are preferred for bread. Milk fat and butter are beta-prime; sunflower, soybean and coconut oils are generally beta. *[literature, medium; novita-0832; [7vLYCrhbgaU 9:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7vLYCrhbgaU&t=574s); 2024-07 — cites doi:10.1016/j.jcs.2011.08.011]*
- Oil in bread gives a moister crumb but lower volume and a denser, harder loaf, so use oil with caution in pan breads where volume matters. *[creator-rule, medium; novita-0833; [7vLYCrhbgaU 11:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7vLYCrhbgaU&t=685s); 2024-07]*
- In a pan-bread test, olive oil gave hardness and springiness similar to shortening, but lower loaf volume. *[literature, medium; novita-0834; [7vLYCrhbgaU 11:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7vLYCrhbgaU&t=716s); 2024-07]*
- Butter for soft sandwich rolls is about 6% of flour, up to 6–8% for softer rolls, and is added after windowpane gluten has developed. *[creator-rule, high; novita-0355; [UK88XjySuZA 17:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UK88XjySuZA&t=1033s); 2025-03]*
- A cold ferment at about 5 °C slows lipid oxidation, which reduces stale or off flavours from endogenous lipoxygenase activity in flour. *[extrapolation, low; novita-0310; [6U-O-lALgK4 2:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6U-O-lALgK4&t=179s); 2025-08]*
- Her heavy-cream trick at about 10 minutes is presented as improving texture. *[anecdote, low; novita-0602; [ySICQ6Twwx8 18:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ySICQ6Twwx8&t=1127s); 2026-09]*
- For a light, fluffy loaf use less tangzhong and add solid fat (butter or shortening), which can increase bread volume. *[creator-rule, medium; novita-0016; [ocBV6-O5SL4 19:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ocBV6-O5SL4&t=1179s); 2026-10]*

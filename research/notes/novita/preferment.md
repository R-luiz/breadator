# preferment — Novita Listyani / Seraphine Lishe

Paraphrased from the creators' videos (all rights reserved; facts only). Every bullet links to a claim in `data/claims-novita.jsonl` (id, video, timestamp, evidence label). Dates are video publication dates — her methods changed over time.

## Synthesis

- **Poolish** (100 % hydration) is her default, almost always combined with a tangzhong. How she runs it has changed: 2020–21 fruit-yeast-water poolish, 10–18 h at room temperature until concave or it floats → 2022–23 0.5–1 % instant yeast on poolish flour (or about 0.2 % of total flour), about 2 h at 27–28 °C then fridge overnight, with extra yeast in the final dough → 2026 XML: 1 h at room temperature, then fridge overnight, and **no extra yeast** in the final dough (longer bulk). A same-day option is ~0.2 % of total flour as yeast, about 3 h at 27 °C to peak. 0.5 g yeast on 50 g flour peaks in about 3.5 h at 27 °C.
- **Readiness cues:** domed, bubbly-active surface, sweet-tangy smell. A "tide mark" on the glass means it has already peaked.
- **Prefermented flour:** 20–45 % typical; up to 50 %.
- **Refrigerated sponge (RSD, 2026):** for 100 % whole wheat, ferment 45–50 % of the flour as a 100 % hydration poolish with about 1.2 % yeast on that flour, 18 h in the fridge. Resistant starch rises from 7.74 % to 13.8 % (sourdough 14.75 %), and the loaf is the softest and fluffiest of those tested. It browns less, so an egg wash helps.
- **Biga** (2:1 flour:water, 1 % yeast, 30–45 min then fridge) appears in a few enriched breads.
- **Disagreement over time:** in 2024 she says cold-retarding the final dough after a poolish is unnecessary. In 2025 she started retarding final doughs (quick cold ferment).

### preferment

- Grape or fruit yeast water is fermented at room temperature for 6 to 10 hours before use in dough; in warm climates bulk can take about 6 hours. *[creator-rule, low; novita-0346; [PcP7fM9jG3s 0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PcP7fM9jG3s&t=0s); 2020-02]*
- A poolish of equal weights bread flour and fruit yeast water ferments 10 to 18 h at room temperature until bubbly with a slightly concave surface. *[creator-rule, high; novita-0285; [e-muI4j42F8 description](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=e-muI4j42F8); 2020-05]*
- A poolish of equal weights bread flour and fruit yeast water, matured 12-18 h at room temperature until domed and dimpled (15 h shown), is the starter for this no-knead herb loaf. *[creator-rule, medium; novita-0697; [jRUssJBe4Xk 1:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jRUssJBe4Xk&t=82s); 2020-07]*

- Mantou (steamed bread) quantities 'Chinese-style' use 2 g dry yeast to 200 g flour (1%). *[creator-rule, high; novita-0407; [1Wwvxu8C8pk description](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1Wwvxu8C8pk); 2021-05]*

### preferment.levain

- A levain of 20 g starter, 40 g flour and 40 g water that rises to at least double in 5 to 6 h gives leavening power comparable to about 100 g of starter. It also lets the baker keep a small starter and change its flavour. *[creator-rule, medium; novita-0729; [qpE_Hz0fJTM 3:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qpE_Hz0fJTM&t=182s); 2023-10]*

### preferment.poolish

- Fruit-yeast-water poolish for no-knead ciabatta: 10–18 h at room temperature, or refrigerate up to 2 days; ready when the surface has flattened and then slightly sunk (concave). *[creator-rule, high; novita-0214; [7LuO4wfL-uI 1:23–2:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7LuO4wfL-uI&t=83s); 2020-04]*
- Focaccia poolish: 100 g bread flour and 100 g fruit yeast water, left 10–18 h; ready when it is slightly concave on the surface. It can be refrigerated up to 2 days before use. *[creator-rule, high; novita-0219; [0M8A_szL2kI 1:18–2:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0M8A_szL2kI&t=78s); 2020-04]*
- Fruit-yeast-water poolish: equal weights of bread flour and fruit yeast water, fermented 10 to 18 h covered, to leaven the dough without instant yeast. *[creator-rule, medium; novita-0787; [T9SdsTAILj4 0:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=T9SdsTAILj4&t=39s); 2020-07]*
- Fruit-yeast-water poolish: equal weights bread flour and fruit yeast water, left 12–18 h to ferment; ready when bubbly and it floats in water (float test). *[creator-rule, high; novita-0199; [wl7zl__jSGI 1:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wl7zl__jSGI&t=100s); 2020-09]*
- Poolish for milk bread: equal weights bread flour and water with 1/4 tsp instant yeast (about 1.2 g), mixed water first, then yeast, then flour; left about 2 h at roughly 28 °C. Colder conditions need more time or a little more yeast. *[creator-rule, high; novita-0165; [iPaBk4U33sM 3:34–4:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=iPaBk4U33sM&t=214s); 2023-06]*
- Tangzhong lengthens the mixing time needed because the colloidal starch takes longer to incorporate; poolish shortens mixing time and improves extensibility. *[creator-rule, medium; novita-0167; [iPaBk4U33sM 6:44–7:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=iPaBk4U33sM&t=404s); 2023-06]*
- A 100%-hydration poolish fermented for about 2 h and then refrigerated overnight is used for flavour rather than gas. Extra instant yeast is therefore added to the final dough. *[creator-rule, medium; novita-0765; [jMBp7YD3ArM 3:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jMBp7YD3ArM&t=212s); 2023-06]*
- For a one-day or overnight ciabatta, a poolish at 100% hydration is preferred over a biga because it ferments faster. Ferment it with a tiny yeast dose and use it at its peak, when it has risen highest and begins to recede, for best flavour and gassing power. *[creator-rule, medium; novita-0758; [osJPNun9IHg 5:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=osJPNun9IHg&t=329s); 2023-08]*
- Overnight poolish for pretzels: 100 g bread flour, 100 g water and 0.5 g instant yeast (0.5% of poolish flour), left about 2 h at roughly 27–28 °C, then refrigerated overnight. Very small yeast amounts keep overnight poolish from over-fermenting in warm rooms. *[creator-rule, medium; novita-0157; [wqqDlDgVJJ0 10:25–11:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wqqDlDgVJJ0&t=625s); 2023-09]*
- Poolish for ciabatta: 100 g bread flour, 100 g water and 0.6 g instant yeast (0.2% of total flour), mixed with a fork until no dry flour is left, left about 1 h at room temperature, then refrigerated overnight for flavour. *[creator-rule, high; novita-0194; [3x4p_bECQbM 5:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3x4p_bECQbM&t=312s); 2026-01]*
- Overnight poolish (and tangzhong) rest in the fridge is her key flavour step for ciabatta; she says doing it a few hours before mixing also works, but the overnight rest improves flavour. *[creator-rule, medium; novita-0198; [3x4p_bECQbM 0:55; 6:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3x4p_bECQbM&t=55s); 2026-01]*
- For the 18-hour method the whole wheat poolish (100% hydration, ~1.2% instant yeast on poolish flour) goes straight into the fridge for 18 h; she has fermented preferment and dough longer with good results. *[creator-rule, high; novita-0035; [DtsBfGiq1H0 1:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DtsBfGiq1H0&t=97s); 2026-08]*
- Poolish mixing order: whisk the instant yeast into the dry flour first, then add the water; mixing takes under a minute. *[creator-rule, high; novita-0036; [DtsBfGiq1H0 1:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DtsBfGiq1H0&t=60s); 2026-08]*

# staling-storage — Novita Listyani / Seraphine Lishe

Paraphrased from the creators' videos (all rights reserved; facts only). Every bullet links to a claim in `data/claims-novita.jsonl` (id, video, timestamp, evidence label). Dates are video publication dates — her methods changed over time.

## Synthesis

- Staling is mostly starch retrogradation plus loss of crumb moisture. Tangzhong delays it through more water and shorter amylopectin chains; the hydration of the final dough governs retrogradation.
- **Crispness:** crusts that crack on cooling (quick move to a rack) and lighter steam stay crisp longer. A crust loses crispness above about 10 % moisture.
- **Bread freezing:** she freezes baked whole wheat bread and reheats it after a spritz of water.
- **Trehalose** (5 %): softer crumb, slower firming, a pale crust; it is not fermentable.

## Claims (18)


### staling-storage

- Stale youtiao can be re-crisped in an oven at 180 °C for 5 to 10 min. *[creator-rule, medium; novita-0798; [p5pLU6y0CpE 9:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=p5pLU6y0CpE&t=584s); 2020-06]*
- The yudane method helps the bread keep soft and retain moisture for longer. *[creator-rule, medium; novita-0292; [zBTHt0uWUeM description](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zBTHt0uWUeM); 2021-03]*
- Starch retrogradation during ageing is controlled by the water present during ageing, regardless of the water used in gelatinisation; so the final-dough water outside the tangzhong matters for staling. *[literature, medium; novita-0828; [HJ6LMqWrZp8 9:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HJ6LMqWrZp8&t=589s); 2023-05 — cites doi:10.3136/fstr.20.1071]*
- Bread with honey is sweeter the next day, and should be left to cool fully before cutting. *[anecdote, low; novita-0542; [FVE0zv6nam4 17:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FVE0zv6nam4&t=1028s); 2023-05]*
- These buns stay soft and get slightly sweeter over time, so they can be made ahead. *[anecdote, low; novita-0771; [jMBp7YD3ArM 15:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jMBp7YD3ArM&t=953s); 2023-06]*
- In a basic dough, adding trehalose increased specific volume, reduced crumb hardness and firming rate, and improved sensory quality compared with a control, but yeast cannot ferment trehalose, so it cannot fully replace sugar. *[literature, low; novita-0437; [KQhgxDLsIhU 11:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KQhgxDLsIhU&t=669s); 2023-06]*
- Well-made enriched white bread like shokupan stays soft and fresh longer and tastes better the next day. *[anecdote, low; novita-0763; [5dfm-ZiBfMc 13:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5dfm-ZiBfMc&t=825s); 2024-04]*
- In one Philippine study, adding freshly crushed garlic to bread extended shelf life by inhibiting mould growth. *[literature, low; novita-0666; [VXyp8CVj3hw 2:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VXyp8CVj3hw&t=143s); 2024-06]*
- Water activity is what salt lowers; typical bread sits at about 88 to 95% equilibrium relative humidity, which makes it prone to microbial spoilage. Salt is about 11 times more effective than sugar at lowering water activity in equal amounts. *[creator-rule, medium; novita-0737; [f6suuWjPSF4 6:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=f6suuWjPSF4&t=390s); 2024-08]*
- Lowering salt in a lean bread from 1.2% to 0.6%, 0.3% and 0.0% shortened shelf life by about 2 days (from about 5-6 days to 3-4 days) under controlled conditions. The bread was exposed to the bakery air for only 10 min before bagging. *[literature, medium; novita-0738; [f6suuWjPSF4 7:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=f6suuWjPSF4&t=462s); 2024-08 — cites doi:10.1007/s00253-012-4052-x]*
- With sourdough, shelf life extended to 12 to 14 days compared with the control, and the salt level no longer significantly changed shelf life. The study used a specific antifungal, so results may vary with starter. *[literature, medium; novita-0739; [f6suuWjPSF4 8:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=f6suuWjPSF4&t=533s); 2024-08 — cites doi:10.1007/s00253-012-4052-x]*
- In a study replacing 20% of wheat flour with high-quality cassava flour, a sensory panel accepted the blend. Cassava breads had higher bacterial and fungal loads, higher pH and moisture, and probably shorter shelf life than 100% wheat bread. *[literature, medium; novita-0750; [Du35FWaSo1U 2:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Du35FWaSo1U&t=139s); 2024-10 — cites doi:10.3746/pnf.2014.19.2.115]*
- Heavy steam (especially with long baking) lowers crust water-vapour permeability; the denser crust traps crumb moisture so crispness is lost faster in storage (crust loses crispness above ~10% moisture). The paper recommended medium steam as a compromise. *[literature, medium; novita-0090; [1OD5Kex5v4g 12:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1OD5Kex5v4g&t=726s); 2025-06 — cites doi:10.1016/j.jfoodeng.2011.07.015, doi:10.1016/j.jcs.2012.11.006]*
- Some crust starch gelatinization increased crispness retention (via cracks and permeability) but there was no further benefit beyond 75% crust gelatinization (non-steamed composite doughs). *[literature, medium; novita-0091; [1OD5Kex5v4g 13:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1OD5Kex5v4g&t=796s); 2025-06 — cites doi:10.1016/j.lwt.2019.05.094]*
- Crack-promoting crusts stayed crisp up to eight times longer in the first hours; the study used special doughs, so home results may differ. *[literature, low; novita-0439; [8fxvbLUJji8 9:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8fxvbLUJji8&t=572s); 2025-06 — cites doi:10.1016/j.jcs.2012.11.006]*
- Move freshly baked bread to a cool place such as a wire rack or fridge briefly to create thermal shock that promotes cracking. *[creator-rule, medium; novita-0440; [8fxvbLUJji8 10:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8fxvbLUJji8&t=610s); 2025-06]*
- Higher moisture content in bread gives a softer crumb and slower firming, because staling is largely the crumb losing moisture and becoming firm. *[literature, medium; novita-0650; [gPYzcoOgatI 7:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gPYzcoOgatI&t=475s); 2025-07]*

### staling-storage.freezing

- She freezes the baked whole wheat bread to keep it and (per her reading of the literature) further raise resistant starch; reheat in microwave or oven after a spritz of water so it does not dry out. *[creator-rule, low; novita-0030; [GZEvf4buPf4 7:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GZEvf4buPf4&t=429s); 2026-08 — cites doi:10.1016/j.jff.2014.11.023]*

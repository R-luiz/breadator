# bulk — Novita Listyani / Seraphine Lishe

Paraphrased from the creators' videos (all rights reserved; facts only). Every bullet links to a claim in `data/claims-novita.jsonl` (id, video, timestamp, evidence label). Dates are video publication dates — her methods changed over time.

## Claims (26)


### bulk

- Palm wine (tuak) is a natural yeast source; bika ambon ferments 8 h overnight so yeast builds honeycomb structure before baking. *[creator-rule, medium; novita-0419; [qevAP88CBCQ 2:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qevAP88CBCQ&t=159s); 2019-08]*
- Bulk with two stretch-and-fold sessions: rest 20 min, degas and fold, rest another 20 min. *[creator-rule, medium; novita-0527; [bqVU4XqETno 6:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bqVU4XqETno&t=387s); 2020-01]*
- Fruit yeast water: a poolish of 100 g flour and 100 ml fruit yeast water fermented 10 to 18 h at room temperature is her base. *[creator-rule, medium; novita-0516; [mnWtBZWISFA 0:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mnWtBZWISFA&t=37s); 2020-05]*
- Cold bulk: after an hour at room temperature, refrigerate the dough overnight; the slower yeast activity lets enzymes build more complex flavour. *[creator-rule, high; novita-0203; [wl7zl__jSGI 7:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wl7zl__jSGI&t=444s); 2020-09]*
- Bulk about 5 h or until doubled, depending on room temperature; a warmer room is faster. *[creator-rule, medium; novita-0520; [fBtJ2ckJ9Vc 7:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=fBtJ2ckJ9Vc&t=429s); 2020-10]*
- Yeasted muffin batter with poolish: after mixing, ferment 4 h at 26 C or 10-16 h in the fridge until it looks bubbly and aerated; the 26 C room is a condition of the 4 h time. *[creator-rule, medium; novita-0857; [XotTKXl3NQ4 4:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XotTKXl3NQ4&t=293s); 2020-11]*
- Overnight cold retard in the fridge develops flavour through enzyme activity; the dough can be wrapped and refrigerated in a basket until baking. *[creator-rule, high; novita-0226; [4Cpsgir28HM 8:44–9:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4Cpsgir28HM&t=524s); 2021-03]*
- Bulk ferment about 1 to 1.5 hours until nearly doubled, judged by the dough reaching the bowl's midline. *[creator-rule, high; novita-0325; [ACHbkOi8esY 8:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ACHbkOi8esY&t=482s); 2021-11]*
- Bulk fermentation uses four sets of stretch-and-folds (four per set) 45 min apart; total bulk runs about 3-5 h depending on room temperature, fruit yeast water activity and target dough temperature. Stop folding when the dough gets hard to stretch. *[creator-rule, medium; novita-0703; [qy3eJV8A9ks 2:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qy3eJV8A9ks&t=133s); 2022-01]*
- Yeast can be cut for longer fermentation, but table sugar speeds yeast up, so the sugar in the recipe must be counted. *[creator-rule, high; novita-0273; [Evt_8_sl29o 8:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Evt_8_sl29o&t=511); 2022-05]*
- Bulk fermentation after mixing is about 1 hour before dividing. *[creator-rule, medium; novita-0586; [1R62RANFg4Q 12:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1R62RANFg4Q&t=728s); 2022-10]*
- Milk makes the dough less acidic and slows fermentation, but gives better temperature tolerance; to compensate she adds more instant yeast (half a teaspoon) and a little sugar. *[creator-rule, medium; novita-0839; [qXhVxe1DDXE 7:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qXhVxe1DDXE&t=453s); 2023-04]*
- Bulk fermentation is done when the dough has about doubled, which she checks by the dough reaching roughly the halfway mark on a scraper line or the pan-rim height; she gives about 1 h as the bulk time. *[creator-rule, medium; novita-0488; [qdOHmdTTs24 20:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qdOHmdTTs24&t=1225s); 2023-04]*
- Shortened bulk for poolish and instant yeast doughs: about 30 min until the dough is about 1.5 times its original volume. Shorten bulk when the preferment is active and the room is warm, and compensate for the time needed to shape many pieces. *[creator-rule, medium; novita-0171; [iPaBk4U33sM 9:32–10:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=iPaBk4U33sM&t=572s); 2023-06]*
- Doubling is judged by pushing the dough to the midline of the bowl; the dough is doubled when it reaches that height again. *[creator-rule, high; novita-0307; [hd-vdRnb-JI 13:29](https://www.youtube.com/timestamp/hd-vdRnb-JI); 2023-10]*
- Bulk fermentation about 45 to 60 min until the dough doubles. *[creator-rule, medium; novita-0499; [QuhM5qSQ7T0 15:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QuhM5qSQ7T0&t=936s); 2023-11]*
- Yeast uses sucrose before maltose, so a sugar-rich dough keeps some maltose in the finished loaf. *[literature, low; novita-0500; [QuhM5qSQ7T0 27:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QuhM5qSQ7T0&t=1660s); 2023-11]*
- Bulk about 45 to 60 min to double, or the dough can be retarded overnight in the fridge instead. *[creator-rule, medium; novita-0546; [txzOxC86ePU 9:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=txzOxC86ePU&t=597s); 2023-11]*
- Bulk is about 45–60 min; the proof is about 45–60 min for this enriched dough. *[creator-rule, high; novita-0403; [LewGFXECOe4 10:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LewGFXECOe4&t=601s); 2024-01]*
- Most kombucha studies ferment at 22–30 °C; at 30 °C more sugar is consumed and more acid is made than at 22 °C, and temperature selects which acetic acid bacteria dominate. *[literature, medium; novita-0178; [0RHwNzwXG_s 15:09–15:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0RHwNzwXG_s&t=943s); 2024-02]*
- Soft dough (shokupan, buns, rolls) with optimal hydration: knead to peak elasticity, then bulk about 45 min at about 30 °C, which is the middle of the 30–60 min rest range; strain hardening continues through bulk, shaping and oven spring. *[creator-rule, medium; novita-0189; [n_bwkqy93zs 14:42–15:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=n_bwkqy93zs&t=882s); 2025-01]*
- Bulk fermentation is about 1 h, until nearly doubled. *[creator-rule, high; novita-0360; [UK88XjySuZA 25:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UK88XjySuZA&t=1532s); 2025-03]*
- An open crumb needs enough fermentation, more water and a weaker gluten network, plus gentle handling; adding dense ingredients makes the crumb less open. *[creator-rule, medium; novita-0657; [gPYzcoOgatI 21:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gPYzcoOgatI&t=1308s); 2025-07]*
- Cold fermentation alone has not been shown to raise volume (white-bread volume rose with yeast dose, time and temperature), but dough mixed and bulk-fermented at 17 °C gave the highest loaf and specific volume versus 23 °C and 30 °C; mix cold, bulk in the fridge and proof warm for flavour plus volume. *[literature, medium; novita-0083; [CClhjpjcAd8 20:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CClhjpjcAd8&t=1243s); 2025-08 — cites doi:10.1007/s00217-007-0668-y, doi:10.1007/s00217-003-0717-0]*
- Bulk ferment about 1.5 h at (her warm) room temperature until doubled; longer in cooler rooms, or in the fridge. *[creator-rule, medium; novita-0039; [DtsBfGiq1H0 3:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DtsBfGiq1H0&t=238s); 2026-08]*

### bulk.gas-cells

- Dough air is about 4 to 15% of volume after mixing, up to about 20%; a baked loaf is about 75 to 85% gas by volume. *[literature, medium; novita-0559; [dtBqv-qIFLo 1:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dtBqv-qIFLo&t=108s); 2025-05 — cites doi:10.1002/cche.10699]*

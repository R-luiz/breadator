# nutrition — Novita Listyani / Seraphine Lishe

Paraphrased from the creators' videos (all rights reserved; facts only). Every bullet links to a claim in `data/claims-novita.jsonl` (id, video, timestamp, evidence label). Dates are video publication dates — her methods changed over time.

## Claims (14)


### nutrition

- Adding wheat bran increases dietary fibre in bread and gives a slightly sweet, nutty flavour. *[anecdote, low; novita-0468; [WUilMHvvpCk 0:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WUilMHvvpCk&t=45s); 2022-07]*
- Salt at about 1.7% of flour is roughly 1% of the final dough weight, close to the common health guideline for salt in bread. *[creator-rule, low; novita-0547; [txzOxC86ePU 23:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=txzOxC86ePU&t=1398s); 2023-11]*
- Safety: the CDC is cited as saying about 100 g of kombucha a day poses no risk to a healthy adult; overconsumption can be risky, and people with weakened immunity should consult a doctor. *[literature, low; novita-0180; [0RHwNzwXG_s 11:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0RHwNzwXG_s&t=662s); 2024-02]*
- Human evidence for kombucha health benefits is very limited: a 2022 review found only 3 clinical trials since 2018; a 2023 study on diabetic subjects found lower fasting glucose after 4 weeks, but the sample was small and attrition high. *[literature, medium; novita-0181; [0RHwNzwXG_s 8:36–10:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0RHwNzwXG_s&t=516s); 2024-02]*
- Sourdough bread has not yet been shown to have clinically established health benefits over yeast-leavened bread; EFSA and FDA have not approved any sourdough health claim. *[literature, high; novita-0145; [bIP-8B2P1A8 0:37–1:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bIP-8B2P1A8&t=37s); 2024-05 — cites doi:10.3389/fnut.2023.1230043, doi:10.1016/j.advnut.2022.10.003]*
- 'Better digestibility' of sourdough bread is not supported: studies on satiety and gastric emptying give conflicting results, and in 5 studies comparing sourdough with yeast bread no satiety difference was found. *[literature, medium; novita-0146; [bIP-8B2P1A8 5:55–8:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bIP-8B2P1A8&t=355s); 2024-05 — cites doi:10.1016/j.advnut.2022.10.003]*
- The claim that sourdough bread has a lower glycaemic index than yeast bread is not conclusively supported; more than 50% of sourdough vs white wheat bread comparisons found no significant glycaemic difference in healthy people. *[literature, high; novita-0151; [bIP-8B2P1A8 14:48–16:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bIP-8B2P1A8&t=888s); 2024-05 — cites doi:10.1016/j.advnut.2022.10.003]*
- Fructans and other FODMAPs are the trigger for IBS symptoms in some people; sourdough fermentation can reduce FODMAPs in vitro, but clinical benefit for IBS patients is unclear and depends on overall diet. *[literature, medium; novita-0153; [bIP-8B2P1A8 34:00–43:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bIP-8B2P1A8&t=2040s); 2024-05]*
- Adding garlic powder reduced acrylamide: 5 g garlic powder in 500 g wheat flour (1% of flour) gave a 45% reduction against a control, and more garlic gave more reduction; allicin and flavonoids are named as the active components. *[literature, medium; novita-0667; [VXyp8CVj3hw 4:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VXyp8CVj3hw&t=248s); 2024-06]*
- Biscuits made with spent coffee grounds increased satiety and reduced ad libitum energy intake in healthy overweight volunteers in a pilot study; biscuits were well tolerated. *[literature, medium; novita-0693; [qPNc-28unnA 1:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qPNc-28unnA&t=103s); 2024-06 — cites doi:10.1111/jfbc.13204]*
- Health benefits attributed to sourdough may come from other ingredients such as whole grain, fibre or ancient grains rather than the sourdough itself. Sourdough-labelled industrial loaves with a little starter and much sugar are unlikely to be healthier. *[literature, medium; novita-0720; [F2nGI0vgpCw 21:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=F2nGI0vgpCw&t=1298s); 2026-01 — cites doi:10.3389/fnut.2023.1230043]*
- Whole wheat contains 9–17% dietary fibre, of which only about 34% is fermentable by gut bacteria. *[literature, medium; novita-0029; [GZEvf4buPf4 2:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GZEvf4buPf4&t=124s); 2026-08 — cites doi:10.1016/j.jff.2014.11.023]*

### nutrition.resistant-starch

- In the cited study, whole wheat bread resistant starch was 7.74% with straight (~2 h warm) fermentation, 13.8% with refrigerated sponge dough and 14.75% with sourdough (RSD vs sourdough not significantly different); RSD gave the softest, fluffiest bread even compared with sourdough, and both RSD and sourdough breads had a lower glycaemic index. *[literature, high; novita-0025; [GZEvf4buPf4 4:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GZEvf4buPf4&t=293s); 2026-08 — cites doi:10.1002/jsfa.12565]*
- Thresholds proposed for a beneficial resistant-starch intake vary by author: at least 14% of total starch, 6 g per meal, or 20 g per day; resistant starch is starch not digested within 120 min. *[literature, medium; novita-0028; [GZEvf4buPf4 3:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GZEvf4buPf4&t=193s); 2026-08 — cites doi:10.1016/j.jff.2022.105094]*

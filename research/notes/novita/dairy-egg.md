# dairy-egg — Novita Listyani / Seraphine Lishe

Paraphrased from the creators' videos (all rights reserved; facts only). Every bullet links to a claim in `data/claims-novita.jsonl` (id, video, timestamp, evidence label). Dates are video publication dates — her methods changed over time.

## Claims (18)


### dairy-egg

- Cream of tartar acidifies the egg white foam and helps stabilize it; lemon juice or vinegar are substitutes. *[creator-rule, medium; novita-0474; [RKx0fmHGCSI 4:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RKx0fmHGCSI&t=257s); 2020-03]*
- Milk powder adds milk solids and flavour without adding much liquid; adding the liquid milk first helps the poolish dissolve. *[creator-rule, medium; novita-0707; [J4lyiJjeeH4 2:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=J4lyiJjeeH4&t=122s); 2020-09]*
- Eggs begin to cook at about 60 °C, so the choux paste should be cooled before eggs are beaten in. *[creator-rule, medium; novita-0603; [9OX9XNzoNXI 3:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9OX9XNzoNXI&t=206s); 2020-10]*
- The amount of egg a choux needs varies with humidity, flour and stove, so add egg gradually until the target consistency is reached. *[creator-rule, high; novita-0605; [9OX9XNzoNXI 5:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9OX9XNzoNXI&t=312s); 2020-10]*
- Lactic acid from yogurt flavours bread and may extend shelf life; she adds that acidity affects gluten development and softens the crumb. *[anecdote, low; novita-0465; [kYAu64_J5UM 1:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kYAu64_J5UM&t=76s); 2022-06]*
- Heating milk to 80 to 85 C denatures proteins to thicken the yogurt and reduces competing bacteria; cool to about 40 C before adding starter, then incubate about 3 hours. *[creator-rule, medium; novita-0466; [kYAu64_J5UM 8:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kYAu64_J5UM&t=532s); 2022-06]*
- Egg white is about 90% water and 10% protein with no fat. Yolk is about 50% water, with fats, proteins and lecithin, a natural emulsifier. Yolk fat tenderizes and increases volume, which is why yolk-only recipes are richer and fluffier. *[creator-rule, medium; novita-0799; [uxlYDs03HfI 1:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uxlYDs03HfI&t=67s); 2022-08]*
- Replacing about 25% of the water with milk raises water absorption and dough elasticity; milk also brings sugars and some acidity. *[literature, low; novita-0484; [qdOHmdTTs24 14:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qdOHmdTTs24&t=892s); 2023-04]*
- Whole eggs increase dough strength and bread volume, and their interaction with salt makes eggs more functional in these roles. *[creator-rule, medium; novita-0766; [jMBp7YD3ArM 0:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jMBp7YD3ArM&t=30s); 2023-06]*
- Salt shields the positive charges on gluten proteins, tightening the network; bread dough at pH 5.0 to 6.5 keeps gluten net positive. *[creator-rule, medium; novita-0249; [jP_qyhKPQKY 3:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jP_qyhKPQKY&t=186s); 2023-06]*
- Whole egg strengthens dough and increases its stability; egg white lengthens development time and gives stronger dough, while yolk shortens development time and reduces stability. *[literature, medium; novita-0250; [jP_qyhKPQKY 8:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jP_qyhKPQKY&t=493s); 2023-06]*
- Yolk plasma (LDL fraction) gave a taller loaf than shortening: at 2.8% yolk vs 2.0% shortening, height was about 13% greater. *[measured, low; novita-0251; [jP_qyhKPQKY 17:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jP_qyhKPQKY&t=1033s); 2023-06]*
- Ovalbumin is stable at room temperature and may interact with glutenin at high temperature, which the cited paper links to higher maximum dough height. *[literature, low; novita-0252; [jP_qyhKPQKY 19:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jP_qyhKPQKY&t=1150s); 2023-06]*
- Lecithin (egg) reduces dough stability but does not change development time. *[literature, low; novita-0253; [jP_qyhKPQKY 12:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jP_qyhKPQKY&t=723s); 2023-06]*
- Milk can replace under 25% of the water in a dough: this improves elasticity, chewiness and resilience; she caps the milk share at that level. *[literature, medium; novita-0821; [zClatNjlIPc 9:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zClatNjlIPc&t=565s); 2023-07]*
- Ovalbumin in egg reduces oil absorption in fried dough. *[literature, low; novita-0266; [-ajZk1KPwO0 6:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-ajZk1KPwO0&t=360s); 2023-07]*
- Calcium from milk inhibits phytate hydrolysis, so a dough with milk breaks down phytic acid less. *[literature, low; novita-0504; [Pf5HSzBK6zo 11:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Pf5HSzBK6zo&t=701s); 2024-01]*
- Milk powder can be used as a fresh-milk substitute: 1 g milk powder plus 7.5 g water equals 7.5 g fresh milk. *[creator-rule, high; novita-0398; [aLYDjuEHrT0 1:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=aLYDjuEHrT0&t=71s); 2024-01]*
